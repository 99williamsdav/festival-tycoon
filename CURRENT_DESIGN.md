## What the player can do now

The ordinary New Game route creates a fresh seeded **Build** campaign on the inherited Lower Wittering farm. Tier 1 has 20 mandatory adult guests and a total active-person bound of 50. The player chooses a perk, edits the site, books three distinct acts, hires a qualifying worker, considers equipment and stock, and confirms all charges once at Start. The field, farmhouse/barns, trailer stage and generator are fixed; six movable service kinds begin unplaced. Defaults supply a normal-cost £300 six-service layout. Current Build limits allow two taps and two toilets and one each of food van, bar, first aid and steward post. Movables can be placed, moved and removed within validated grass, access and budget rules. A failed edition returns its last layout and other choices as an editable unpaid plan; owned durable property and perks persist under their own rules.

Preparation uses a persistent bottom dock with **Build, Programme, Staff, Equipment, Stock** above the budget/Start row. Its warning badges and Before opening checklist reflect actual blockers, including water, toilet, safety, three booked acts, worker and budget. Build still exposes **Site & water** management. Selecting a panel or placing an item does not force a wizard step. X collapses a panel; comma/period rotate an active placement, Esc cancels; object-specific actions live in the picked context. The dark top HUD, cream paperwork and restrained teal selection follow the approved UI direction. The full plan remains unpaid until Start; no action may charge a draft twice.

The current Tier 1 festival is **one eight-minute festival-time day** at 80 simulation ticks per festival second. The exact windows are arrivals 00:00–01:00, Set 1 01:00–02:45, changeover 02:45–03:25, Set 2 03:25–05:10, changeover 05:10–05:50, Set 3 05:50–07:50, closing 07:50–08:00, then physical departure. Three acts use one trailer stage and nine physical performers. This current demo schedule supersedes earlier five-minute slot mockups and the older proposed multi-day weekend length. It does **not** itself prove the older about-ten-real-minute weekend pacing target. The reference minimal-staffing `20260922` timetable case still records a fatality before all three sets; do not present the eight-minute schedule as generally balance-accepted or silently force safety.

Guests receive seed/identity-derived release times during the first 55 festival seconds and varied initial hunger, thirst and toilet need. Pending guests are hidden and inactive until release at the gate; **Attendees** counts only actual physical admission. Staff and performer starts are separate. People travel and form physical queues. Interchangeable taps/toilets compare travel plus physical wait/service, review on a bounded cadence and never claim a remote queue place while approaching; an active service owner is not switched. Taps have their current water-rate and community-sharing rules. The portaloo has an open/free green-circle signal and closed/occupied red-bar signal; a visit fills the 12 L tank by 300 mL or 700 mL, smell affects nearby people beyond 25%, and need relief progresses during actual use. Beer raises toilet need while actually consumed. Food, soft drinks and beer have held-item/consumption states. Staff and performers receive the recorded 50% price reduction; staff do not buy beer. Hired staff return to current posts when idle, with their distinct roles visible. The live HUD has plain fading urgent alerts at top left; pause freezes character animation and game music as well as the simulation.

Equipment, medical and disorder chains can produce an attributable death. A protected guest, worker or performer death ends the attempt and leads to the Council hearing; one scarce Council Favour can retry the same tier. Failure is an intended outcome, not a reason to secretly make a run safe. The existing one-time community water-sharing choice can earn a Favour at an explicit tap-rate cost. Retrying resets cash and consumable stock to that tier's starting baseline while retaining eligible owned durable property and the editable plan. Safe completion requires **every admitted guest to physically leave**, with terminal failure taking precedence on the last-exit tick; the simulation then freezes. This **prototype ends at the local results**. Tier 2 transition, quality rewards, reputation unlocks, profit carryover, victory and endless continuation are not implemented by the current demo ending.

The completed **Newspaper** gives only public rating, guest satisfaction and recorded events. Its current star thresholds are 1 below 20%, 2 below 40%, 3 below 60%, 4 below 80%, 5 at or above 80%, based on final satisfaction of admitted departed guests; an empty sample is unrated. **Accounts** is a separate peer document with recorded product/rate quantities, sold-item costs, paid acts/staff/facilities, operating result and cash reconciliation. It includes staff/performer discount rows and the once-paid Build setup fee. Opening cash is not sales income; purchased stock and capital equipment are cash outflows, while consumed stock enters operating costs. No ticket-revenue row is invented. Tabs and Return to menu stay fixed while documents scroll; a completed run remains frozen and saved until a fresh New Game is chosen.

The perk prototype draws three eligible distinct cards from a uniform eight-perk pool, with one reroll per draft. At most five can be equipped; an authorized retry retains owned perks and offers a new choice. Hands, reroll use, identity and effects are saved; independent fresh seeds can legitimately overlap. Presentation colour/body/hair variety is deterministic from campaign seed and person identity, including current male/female body selection, **without an explicit saved gender field**. No children or gender-based statistics are in scope.

## Boundaries and verification

Current Build saves use versioned authoritative state, 30 real **unpaused** seconds for changed-state background saves plus milestone saves. Ordinary draft staff edits and live boundary saves are ordered/asynchronous where implemented; active write failures can roll back to the last durable state. Incompatible development saves are rejected, left intact and not migrated. Do not add individual-action disk writes or treat a screenshot/export as save correctness. The Windows deliverable is an **EXE plus its adjacent Godot .NET data folder, never a ZIP**.

The latest Accounts [brief and evidence](briefs/R0.05ag-festival-accounts.md) passed 53/53 relevant current-version tests and exported 720p/1080p captures; the [guest-arrival brief](briefs/R0.05af-guest-arrivals-needs.md) records its own focused checks. A broader Accounts run aborted after 137 tests with prior-save checksum and programme-timing failures; the arrival full run was 506/517. Old-save fixture failures fall outside the approved migration policy, while equipment timing, timetable, traffic, shared-world/water and frame-hitch concerns remain open in their recorded bounds. Scripted visual captures and a [staged in-engine video](docs/SHOWCASE_PROVENANCE.md) are not a manual end-to-end human playtest or stable FPS certification. The older 50-person performance exception is provisional; no 200-person rendered capacity is established.

All **new in-game graphics** follow the user-approved concept-first workflow before modelling or graphical implementation. Keep provenance and source. Routine manual QA is not a blanket per-change gate, but user playtest findings and required bounded visual checks still matter. Keep the current focus on a legible, honest Tier 1 demo; use the backlog for review candidates and [distant-future ideas](briefs/FUTURE_IDEAS.md) for discussion, without treating either list as automatic authorization.

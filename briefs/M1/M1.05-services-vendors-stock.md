# M1.05 — Services, vendor ownership and stock

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: provision a bar, free water, toilets, food van and merch so their queues, stock and money behave differently and visibly.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§6, 8, 10, 12–13, 16, 18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–9, 11–12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§2, 4, 10–11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisites: M1.04 and M1.02. Food van v1 is approved but must be integrated/verified; missing prototypes follow [ASSET_GAPS.md](ASSET_GAPS.md).

## Scope

- Festival-owned paid bar and generic merch with stock; independent food vendor with pitch fee and vendor-owned takings; free water; toilet service/resource; bounded bins/waste provision and storage/replenishment.
- Physical queues and existing atomic purchase/reservation lifecycle; paid/free service distinctions and explicit closed/broken/stockout states.
- Planning/operations UI for stock presets/counts, prices, ownership, queue/throughput and vendor report.

Excludes vendor bidding variety/food poisoning, ATM/card expansion beyond existing supported primitives, utilities networks, emergency deliveries and staffing depth (M1.09).

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Festival receives bar/merch sales and pitch fee, never vendor gross takings; free water never charges. All ledgers/stock reconcile.
- Exact-cash, insufficient funds, stockout, duplicate completion, closure and service-boundary save/load paths preserve wallets, stock, reservations and ownership.
- Moving bar/vendor between controlled reachable placements changes physical footfall/sales, not a coin flip; blocked/unreachable service draws none.
- Vendor asset integration has provenance and four-view access/readability evidence; provisional proxies are labelled if dual-approved art is pending.

## Demonstration and stop

Run two controlled layouts through ordinary UI, showing paid festival sale, free water, independent vendor sale/report, stockout and a mid-service save/reload. Stop before autonomous need selection.

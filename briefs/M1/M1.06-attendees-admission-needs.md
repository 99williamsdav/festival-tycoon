# M1.06 — Ticket admission, needs and autonomous adults

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: 20–50 sold adult guests arrive through the gate, choose useful destinations themselves, spend from personal funds and leave without player commands.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§5, 8, 10, 12, 16, 18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–11, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§3–5, 9, 11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisites: M1.05, M1.03 and the accepted shared-world architecture from M1.00.

## Scope

- Admit one adult per eligible sold ticket; preserve distinct sold, arrived, on-site and departed counts.
- Authoritative hunger, thirst, bladder, dirt, mood/basic impatience-aggression, genre affinity, personal cash/funds and sticky autonomous action selection.
- Feasible actions: enter, travel, queue, paid/free service, reconsider, idle/rest and depart. No player attendee controls.
- Public selection card/HUD shows current action, important needs, ticket and spend without exposing hidden exact personality.

Excludes children/families/groups, camping, social/romance, intoxication, advanced payment/ATM and stage listening (M1.07).

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Ticket/person/wallet mapping is one-to-one; capacity admission and exit counts reconcile with no invisible customers or duplicate entry.
- Needs and real travel/wait/payment costs change choices. Sticky intentions prevent oscillation; unreachable/closed/failed destinations are remembered and reconsidered.
- Same seed/actions produce the same decisions/hash. Mid-travel, mid-queue and mid-service saves match uninterrupted results with no lost guest/payment/reservation.
- Invalid ticket, full gate, insufficient funds and no feasible optional destination resolve explicitly; no teleport, fence crossing, remote reservation or silent despawn.

## Demonstration and stop

Run 20 then 50 sold adults from staggered arrival through several services to departure, inspect individuals and save/reload mixed states. Stop before stage attendance/weather/incidents.

# M1.00 — Shared-world crowd feasibility gate

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: prove that 50 full-fidelity adults can share one world, choose among multiple real destinations and render without changing product fidelity—or stop M1 before building on a false foundation.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§2, 5, 10, 18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–6, 10–15; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §§2–4; accepted M0.09/M0.10 results and `reports/M0_ACCEPTANCE.md`. Prerequisite: accepted M0.10 plus explicit approval of the proposed bounded M1 exception in the M1 index.

## Scope

- Build a production-code fixture with one `GameSession`/occupancy world, one entrance/exit, 50 distinct people/tickets/wallets, and multiple simultaneously feasible destinations/queues.
- Preserve 4 festival ticks/second and 80 authoritative ticks/real-second at 1×; off-camera agents run identical logic. Only mesh/animation LOD is allowed.
- On declared hardware, explicitly evaluate `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §13: representative stable 60 FPS at 1×, actual attained 1× and reported 4× behaviour, save under two seconds, load under five seconds, peak memory, and input-stall evidence during save/reports. Report tick/frame percentiles and operational counters separately; unmeasured targets are Unverified, never passes.
- Profile before proposing optimization. Route caching or staggered path requests are eligible only with deterministic/save/collision validation and evidence that the profile identifies them.

Excludes campaign, needs, incidents, aggregate populations, simulation LOD, a structural rewrite and all later M1 gameplay.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- All 50 are active in one shared collision/navigation world; destinations influence one another physically. A repeat with the same seed/commands gives the same authoritative hash.
- Exact person/ticket/wallet counts, reservations, transactions and exit ownership reconcile; no off-camera shortcut or silent despawn.
- Save during mixed travel/queues, reload and match uninterrupted completion/hash.
- Valid routes have no fence crossing, teleport, overlap or recovery. Narrow/wide evidence is labelled fixture-specific.
- Rendered evidence measures a representative active interval, not startup/pre-arrival frames; clock units are the production units.
- **Hard stop:** if any feasibility target is unmet, do not silently accept the exception or start M1.01. Produce hotspots, reproducible evidence and bounded options for coordinator/user decision; do not lower tick rate/fidelity or begin a rewrite by default.

## Demonstration and stop

Export a short 50-person run showing one shared entrance, divergence to at least three destinations, queues and egress, with live performance diagnostics and a save/reload comparison. Stop after the gate report is accepted; do not implement campaign systems.

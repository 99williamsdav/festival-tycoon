# M1.01 — Campaign shell, eight weeks and finance

Status: Implemented — verification pending. Stakeholder outcome: start a named festival on the inherited farm, understand the opening finances and manually advance a deterministic eight-week preparation calendar. Evidence: [`briefs/results/M1.01.md`](../results/M1.01.md).

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§3, 6, 16, 18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–5, 8, 10–12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§1–2, 9, 11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisite: accepted M1.00.

## Scope

- Campaign identity: generated/renamable festival and farm-facing palette/site seed; inherited land/structures.
- Authoritative phases through Planning W8…W1 with a manual Advance Week command, preview of known commitments and an autosave before advance.
- Carry forward the existing first-year finance/loan worked example, including £800 opening loan, spendable cash, committed outgoings, principal/interest distinction and atomic rejection of unfunded commands; record the exact content revision rather than treating it as newly approved balance.
- Incremental normal UI: persistent phase/week, cash, commitments and debt HUD plus a concise weekly preview/digest.

Excludes tickets, acts, placement, live operation, further borrowing, rescue/receivership and balance retuning.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Exactly eight manual advances reach the opening-check boundary; editing time never advances authoritative time.
- Same campaign seed/actions produce identical week digests and hashes; save/reload before an advance does not reroll it.
- Worked ledger balances and distinguishes cash, profit and principal. Purchases cannot create unauthorized negative cash or partial state.
- Invalid phase/duplicate command/insufficient funds fail atomically. Autosave failure leaves the prior good save and prevents an ambiguous advance.
- Campaign identity, phase, week, loan and dismissal state persist and hash.

## Demonstration and stop

Create/rename a festival, inspect £800 loan/cash, make one valid and one rejected commitment, save/reload, and advance all eight weeks through normal UI. Stop at opening check; do not fabricate offers or live guests.

# M1.10 — Opening, live, egress, debrief and second edition

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: play the whole event loop through a useful debrief and begin a second edition with the right things retained.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§3, 6, 13, 16–18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–5, 8–12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§2, 5, 9–11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisites: M1.01–M1.09 and an explicit settlement-insufficiency decision from the M1 index.

## Scope

- Opening check with §3 structural blockers versus warnings; phases Opening → Live → Egress → Settlement → Debrief → next Planning W8.
- Egress until all people leave; bounded stuck handling is reported, never throughput despawn. Settle actual tickets, festival sales, vendor fees, costs, stock and loan separately.
- Debrief attributes at least three material result drivers from real facts and starts edition two retaining cash/debt/land/permanent improvements, appropriate nonperishable inventory and persistent relationships/summary—not prior attendee instances.
- Persistent HUD/toolbars/inspectors, dismissible contextual tips, modest approved/provisional animations and licensed/original ambience/alerts with provenance and muted equivalents.

Excludes full newspaper/prestige/Know-how, sophisticated rescue offers unless explicitly selected, campaign victory, final polish and M2 systems.

## Required pre-brief decision: insufficient settlement cash

Choose either (A) a simple terminal receivership when cash plus already approved credit cannot settle liabilities, preserving the final report/save and offering load-earlier/new-campaign while deferring any rescue contract to M2, or (B) explicitly add the extra M1 scope for one fixed, bounded rescue contract and the decline/exceeded-capacity receivership path from `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §3. Neither option may allow unauthorized negative cash, unlimited automatic credit or a forced bailout. Financial exposure remains an opening warning, not a structural blocker or workaround for deferred settlement behaviour.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Structural impossibility blocks opening; stock, staffing, finance, service and pinch-point problems warn but allow a knowingly poor event. Deadline correction is narrow and explicitly costed if present.
- Pause/1×/2×/4× preserve identical state at identical ticks; HUD shows requested versus attained speed honestly.
- Egress/settlement/debrief reconcile money, inventory and people. Second-edition retention/expiry matches declared rules and survives save/reload at phase boundaries.
- Debrief drivers trace to authoritative facts. Context tips fire on meaningful triggers only, dismiss/show-again state persists, and no forced tutorial/camera occurs.
- Insolvency path follows the approved decision and cannot trap or silently fund the campaign.

## Demonstration and stop

Play opening through second-edition W8 using ordinary UI, including warning-only opening, serious alert, egress, three-driver debrief, retention inspection and phase-boundary reloads. Stop before final multi-seed acceptance.

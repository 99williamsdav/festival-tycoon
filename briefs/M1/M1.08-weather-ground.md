# M1.08 — Saved weather, wear, mud and woodchip

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: planning forecasts become the same saved live weather, and rain plus foot traffic visibly damages routes while placed woodchip makes a measurable difference.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§3, 8–10, 16–18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–7, 9–12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§8, 10–11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisites: M1.06 and M1.04.

## Scope

- Seed one hidden event weather trajectory and saved forecast-error state; expose bounded W8/W4/W2/W1 forecast detail and live rain state.
- Authoritative terrain moisture/wear/mud/contamination needed for M1; traffic modifies wear and rain modifies moisture/mud.
- Woodchip placement/cost/durability as the M1 countermeasure; path cost, speed, dirt and missed-arrival inputs use actual cells.
- Forecast and wear/mud overlays plus code-native/provisional rain/ground visuals verified in all camera views.

Excludes storms/lightning, heatwave medical depth, showers/ponchos, full desire-path art, other hardening materials and final shaders.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Reload never rerolls truth or errors. Same seed/weather/traffic produces identical ground state/hash.
- Under controlled equal demand, wet high traffic creates more mud/dirt/slowness than protected/dry; woodchip improves declared measures without making natural ground forbidden.
- Save mid-rain preserves forecast, trajectory, ground cells, guest dirt/route intention and woodchip state.
- Invalid/overlapping/unfunded placement fails atomically; visual effects do not mutate simulation. Routes retain exact swept collision/no corner cutting.

## Demonstration and stop

Show weekly forecast evolution, then a rendered split/controlled rainy route with busy mud versus woodchip, including overlay metrics and mid-rain reload. Stop before staffing/incidents.

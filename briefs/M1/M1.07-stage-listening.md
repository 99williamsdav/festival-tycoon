# M1.07 — Stage listening and missed-act attribution

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: guests physically attend wanted acts—or visibly miss them because of a real queue, route or need—and the game explains which act and why.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§7, 9–10, 16–18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–7, 9–10, 12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§3, 5, 9, 11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisites: M1.06 and M1.03. Any music must be original/properly licensed with provenance; muted play retains visual indicators.

## Scope

- Stage set clock/state, listening area and actual-listener membership; ranked wanted acts from genre affinity.
- Autonomous choice between needs and upcoming/current acts using travel/wait costs; bounded queue abandonment for a favourite.
- Record attended duration/enjoyment inputs and causal missed-act attribution (act ID plus queue, travel, need or capacity reason).
- Stage/timetable/selected-guest UI shows current audience and recent attended/missed thought; a modest performer/set animation or clear visual indicator communicates that the act is live, including when audio is muted.

Excludes multi-stage clashes, artist walk-offs, sound propagation depth, backstage, schedule rescue actions and final art/audio expansion.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Only people physically in the listening area count; travellers and off-camera guests use identical rules. One person cannot listen to two stages.
- Controlled queue/distance changes alter attendance and missed reasons. The cited act/reason exists in authoritative history; no flavour-only deduction.
- Save during a set/queue and reload preserves set time, listener membership, intention and eventual attribution without double-counted enjoyment.
- Timetable removal/invalid act reference fails safely; same seed/actions/hash remain deterministic.

## Demonstration and stop

Run a set with some guests listening, one abandoning a queue in time and one missing the named act due to a deliberate real delay; inspect causal cards and reload mid-set. Stop before weather/staff incidents.

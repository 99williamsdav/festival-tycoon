# M1.02 — Fixed offers, seeded demand and tickets

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: choose a date, ticket price and real advertised act, then watch eight deterministic weekly sales updates respond to one named competitor.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§3, 5–7, 18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–5, 8, 12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§2–3, 5, 9, 11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisite: M1.01; coordinate the minimum act record with M1.03 without prebuilding its timetable.

## Scope

- Four date choices, one simplified named competitor/date interaction, fixed act offers and seeded finite prospect demand.
- Default ticket price plus supported overrides; one day-pass product, capacity cap and unique buyer/ticket identities.
- Sales unlock only after date, price and **at least one player-confirmed act**. Present 5–7 candidates later via M1.03; never auto-book or fill a programme.
- Weekly sales/digest UI shows offer, competitor, estimates versus actual sold/remaining; sales create future attendee identities/funds without admitting them.

Excludes marketing channels, refunds beyond a narrowly tested invalidation path, overselling, negotiations, families/camping/VIP and off-screen competitor simulation.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- No confirmed act means no sales; confirming one starts seeded sales. Same seed/actions/reload produce the same unique buyers and money.
- Price/date/competitor controlled comparisons change demand for stated reasons; capacity cannot oversell and one ticket maps to one future person.
- Ticket cash enters the festival ledger once; rejected/duplicate week commands and save/reload cannot double sales.
- Unaccepted offers are non-binding; unaffordable confirmation fails atomically. Advertised commitments are retained for later missed/cancellation attribution.

## Demonstration and stop

Compare two controlled date/price plans, confirm one act manually, advance weeks and inspect named-rival effect, tickets and ledger through normal UI. Stop before timetable/stage behaviour.

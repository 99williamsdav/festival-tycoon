## Missing or unverified M1 presentation

- Prototype assets: toilets, free-water point, bins/waste station, medical/first-aid point, security post and small merch point; stock/warehouse readability if the barn cannot carry it alone.
- Role cues: security, medical, cleaning, hospitality/vendor and performer silhouettes or accessories distinguishable at normal zoom.
- Animation verification: attendee walk/wait/listen/cheer/eat-drink/argue or abstract brawl/escort/treatment; staff response; farm gate movement. Root motion must not drive authoritative position.
- Weather/ground verification: rain, wetness/wear/mud/woodchip visual states that remain readable in all four views and do not hide urgent overlays.
- UI verification: planning weeks, offers, timetable, placement/egress, operations, incident alert, debrief and contextual-tip states at 1280×720 plus intended scaling checks.
- Audio: licensed or original generic stage loop(s), crowd/rural/rain ambience and essential interaction/alert cues, each with provenance and muted visual equivalents.
- Optional later polish, not an M1 blocker unless readability fails: farmhouse shadow, more rustic large-barn pass, cute rounded food-van variant, trailer rain cover, asset upgrade variants and expanded cosmetic diversity.

Each owning brief must state whether code-native/provisional presentation is sufficient for its demonstration. Never imply an approved source asset is integrated when provenance says otherwise.

# M1.09 — Staffing, response and bounded incidents

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: hire understandable security, medical and cleaning coverage, then watch real conditions cause a small set of incidents that staff physically respond to.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§10–13, 16–18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–6, 8–12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§4, 8–11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisites: M1.05–M1.08. Missing posts/role cues follow [ASSET_GAPS.md](ASSET_GAPS.md).

## Scope

- Prepaid M1 department totals/coverage for hospitality, security, medical and cleaning; optionally maintenance only where needed for toilet breakdown. Workers/jobs travel in the shared world and cannot be individually commanded/teleported.
- Bounded causal incidents: toilet breakdown, generic injury/first aid, stockout alert, and frustration → argument → possible brawl/security response. Mud delay/missed act reuse M1.07–08 history.
- Job priority, travel, workload, resolution and causal IDs; serious incident auto-pauses once with a valid dispatch/close/treat response.
- Operations coverage, alert and incident inspector UI; modest role cues/provisional animation, never diagnostics-only.

Excludes complex romance, drugs/alcohol, food poisoning, police/legal simulation, staff biographies/shifts and arbitrary drama quotas.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Every incident has real prerequisites/participants/location; removing aggression, fault, injury or exposure changes the chain. Clean/well-covered controls reduce impact/frequency.
- No available medic/security means a visible backlog, not magical resolution. Response time includes travel; priority remains deterministic.
- One serious incident emits one deduplicated auto-pause alert; pause freezes authority while camera/selection and valid response UI work.
- Save during active incident/job preserves causes, responders, reservations and resolution; reload cannot repeat charges/alerts/outcomes.
- Staff/service costs reconcile. No valid response strands egress or silently deletes guests.

## Demonstration and stop

Compare staffed/understaffed controlled fixtures, resolve an injury and an argument through ordinary operations UI, and reload during a response. Stop before full lifecycle/debrief.

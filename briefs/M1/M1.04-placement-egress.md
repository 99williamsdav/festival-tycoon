# M1.04 — Placement, access and emergency egress

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: lay out a small festival with readable footprints/doors/queues while the game prevents structurally impossible access.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§3, 8, 13, 16–18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–7, 10–12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§4, 10–11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisites: M1.00 and M1.01; use accepted runtime farm assets and [ASSET_GAPS.md](ASSET_GAPS.md).

## Scope

- Planning placement/move/rotate for bounded M1 facility footprints, interaction faces, queue areas and service access on the existing hidden grid.
- Shared navigation rebuild/validation and an egress/access overlay. Preserve inherited boundary/gate.
- Opening check follows `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §3: **block only** missing usable entrance/exit, illegal boundary or unreachable occupied area; financial/service/stock/pinch-point shortcomings are warnings that may proceed.
- Save/hash placement and nav-affecting state; normal Site UI with ghosts/conflict reasons and uncommitted undo where supported.

Excludes live relocation, freeform fence editor, utilities networks, additional land and new designer assets.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Valid placements change routes/footfall physically; no arbitrary location score. Entrances/queues never intersect buildings/hedges or cross fences.
- Placement that destroys all emergency egress or makes an occupied area unreachable is atomically rejected or remains an explicit opening blocker.
- A poor but structurally possible layout remains openable with warnings. Save/reload yields identical footprints/nav hashes/routes.
- No valid-layout fixture uses stuck recovery; invalid placement cannot partially charge or corrupt navigation.

## Demonstration and stop

Place/rotate/move stage and service proxies, show one warning-only poor layout and one blocked impossible layout, repair it and run a short route/egress visual. Stop before implementing service economics.

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: a reviewer can fund, plan, run and settle a small one-day festival at 20 and 50 guests without debug controls, with honest save/performance evidence.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read all `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` sections used by M1.00–M1.10, `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–15, `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§1–5 and 8–11, `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §§2–4 and §9, every accepted M1 result, and [ASSET_GAPS.md](ASSET_GAPS.md). ## Scope

- Integrate the normal player UI route: campaign/date/finance, weeks/offers/acts/timetable, placement/services/stock/staff, opening/live/egress/debrief/second edition.
- Run deterministic end-to-end fixtures and rendered playthroughs at 20 and 50 adults across multiple declared seeds, including ordinary and adverse-but-valid layouts/weather.
- Re-measure active shared-world behaviour after all M1 features and explicitly re-evaluate `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §13 at 50 people: representative stable 60 FPS at 1×, actual attained 1× and reported 4×, save under two seconds, load under five seconds, input stalls during save/reports and peak memory, alongside tick/frame percentiles, initialization, routing/recovery, completed people/services and ledger.
- Produce the M1 acceptance matrix mapping every `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4 item to commands, saves, captures and limitations; audit asset/audio provenance and four-view/UI readability.

Excludes M2, scale-target reduction, aggregation/simulation LOD, structural performance rewrite, camping/families/sponsorship/full skill tree and deferred farmhouse/art polish.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Every §4 item in the M1 index coverage table passes at its owner and in integration, or M1 remains Failed/Blocked/Unverified rather than being waived.
- Same seed/actions and 1×/4× equivalent tick schedules converge; week, mid-queue/service, mid-set/weather/incident and phase-boundary saves match uninterrupted outcomes.
- Ledger/tickets/people/wallets/stock/reservations/jobs/incidents reconcile; no debug command, invisible population, remote reservation, fence crossing or silent despawn completes the scenario.
- Rendered 20/50 evidence uses representative active populations in one shared world and reports requested/attained speed honestly. Behaviour additions trigger renewed measurements; M1.00 numbers are not reused as a blanket pass.
- 50 is not called universal capacity. If the final 50 gate fails, stop with profile and bounded recommendations; keep M0 1,200 Fail and future targets unchanged.

## Demonstration and stop

Provide a short recorded five-minute QA route plus saves/captures from complete 20- and 50-person editions over multiple seeds, including a poor-position sales comparison, mud/woodchip, missed act, staff response, debrief and second edition. Stop at M1 acceptance; do not begin M2.

# M1.03 — Candidate acts and one-stage timetable

Status: Draft / Not started. Stakeholder outcome: choose which acts to book and arrange an understandable afternoon/evening programme on the trailer stage.

## Sources and prerequisites

Read `GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md` §§3, 6–7, 13, 16–18; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§3–5, 9–12, 14; `CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md` §§2–3, 9–11; `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4. Prerequisite: accepted M1.02; trailer stage v2 is approved/integrated.

## Scope

- Offer **5–7 selectable candidate acts** with fixed fees/descriptors. Mandatory booking count is not 5–7: one confirmed act is sufficient to start sales, and nothing auto-fills.
- Timetable data is an array of stages for future compatibility but exposes one initial stage; set/changeover blocks, overlap/curfew validation and accessible move controls.
- Confirmation charges exactly once and updates the advertised programme. Minimal stage set state supports later listener integration.
- Incremental act browser/timetable/stage inspector; ordinary interaction, not diagnostics.

Excludes negotiation, multiple exposed stages, clashes, diva behaviour, backstage/hospitality and advanced schedule interventions.

## Acceptance and failure checks

- Candidate generation and availability are deterministic; save/load does not reroll offers or duplicate fees.
- The player may book one, several or none while planning; invalid overlap, out-of-range time, unaffordable booking and duplicate confirmation are rejected atomically.
- Stage-array persistence/hash survives save/load and can deserialize the one-stage programme without assuming a singleton schema.
- Removing/changing a sold commitment is either unavailable in this brief or records the explicit consequence—never silently rewrites buyers' memory.

## Demonstration and stop

Book a subset from 5–7 candidates, deliberately trigger an overlap warning/error, arrange a valid one-stage timetable, save/reload and inspect unchanged fees/order. Stop before audience listening.

# R0.03 — medical causal chain

Status: **Accepted implementation within focused prototype bounds; human playtest and broader pacing remain pending.** Outcome: unmet water/rest exposure creates an avoidable, non-clinical medical emergency. The three exact asset versions in `art/reviews/R0.03/ASSET_APPROVALS.md` were approved before gameplay integration; the rejected first-aid v1 is not integrated.

## Approved scenario direction (24 September 2026)

- Begin with one free drinking-water point and a fixed, visibly **Hot** scenario state. Several guests arrive with staggered but already-elevated thirst, seek water autonomously and create a real physical queue with short per-person refill time. Do not invent paid-water purchases or a full changing-weather system for this slice.
- Attendee decisions compare thirst, heat exposure, travel and expected wait against other desires, including watching the band. Urgency can override entertainment, but guests must not switch goals robotically on the same tick. The selected-person panel shows thirst, heat exposure, current intent and the reason for that choice.
- A guest at the back may become distressed, then collapse. After collapse there is a fixed, visible response window in which a physically dispatched medic can reach and save them; otherwise the chain reaches an attributable death. The incident may use darkly comic “dried up” language without graphic or clinical claims.
- Tune the first scenario around one clearly avoidable casualty and a reachable basic medic response. Do not make simultaneous emergencies impossible for the sole medic to resolve. Numeric need rates, service time, warning/collapse/death windows, starting-thirst distribution and medical capacity remain to be proposed and tested, not silently fixed by this approval.
- Before integration, approve the water-point asset, then first-aid point, then medic visual cues one exact candidate at a time. No unapproved proxy or unrelated asset work.

## Scope

Implement visible need/exposure → distress warning → water/rest, first-aid dispatch or safe removal → collapse → critical untreated state → attributable death. Free water and basic medical response must exist before eligibility. Treatment and safe removal require owned reservations/routes and elapsed response time. Use generic fictional language.

## Acceptance, demonstration and stop

- Counterfactual same-seed tests show each valid timely counter prevents terminal escalation; unavailable route/staff produces a specific actionable cause, never hidden inevitability.
- Queue abandonment, treatment ownership and departure reconcile. Save/load at warning, travel, treatment and critical boundaries matches uninterrupted state.
- Alert deduplicates and first death freezes all other progression. No real clinical claims, graphic presentation or diagnosis simulation.

Demonstrate one prevented route and one labelled fixture escalation with causal inspection. Focus review on eligibility, intervention timing, navigation ownership and persistence. Stop before disorder work.

## User-authorised playtest revision (25 September 2026)

This later decision supersedes the earlier short, fixed per-person refill timing above without changing the accepted causal-chain scope. Move the water point to the open upper-right viewing area away from the stage; arrange a tighter irregular but non-overlapping queue. The one tap serves a person only after physical arrival. Their intent is **Drinking**: thirst and heat exposure fall continuously while they drink, and they leave when thirst reaches zero. Use a distant-readable **WATER** sign without overwriting the approved v3 source asset. Show a collapsed person horizontally during the rescue window, not only after death. Let the water point and first-aid tent be selectable, with useful current state and actions explained in the inspector.

Band members are protected people with visible thirst/heat bars and the same water, overheating, collapse, rescue and death mechanics as guests. Their role-specific need profile must be explicit so future differences can be added without a parallel incident system. Keep the initial set playable; do not proceed to R0.04. The rejected first-aid v1 package remains untouched and outside runtime integration.

## User-authorised water-line correction (25 September 2026)

The earlier compact irregular patch is superseded by one continuous, slightly imperfect tail-to-tap line. Seeking water must not reserve an ordinal place. Admit only on physical arrival at the pipe/queue entrance or current tail, ordered by arrival tick with a deterministic same-tick tie-break; later arrivals go behind those already waiting. The line must advance without collision/pathing stalls, and service still starts only on physical arrival at the front. Demonstrate five or more physically queued people in the default view and a faster farther-starting attendee overtaking a slower earlier departure. Preserve approved v3 art and defer the designer's new sign candidate until exact user approval.

Independent review also requires a full-line overflow correction: once the ten tap-bound places are occupied, a physically arrived visitor keeps their place in a visible, walkable continuation of the line. A later walker must not cut ahead when a main-line place opens. Overflow standing spots advance and promote in arrival order; walkers still en route hold no priority. The bounded continuation refuses new entry when all spots are occupied, rather than silently clustering people at one entrance.

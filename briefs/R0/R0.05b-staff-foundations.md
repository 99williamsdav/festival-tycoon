# R0.05b — staff capacity and individual response

Status: **Implemented — verification pending (coordinator/human handoff).** Direct user authorization on 26 September 2026 covers the coordinator's corrected role-specific slots, role-wide training seams, paid weekend hires, personal abilities, independent assignments and reuse of existing assets. [Implementation and independent review evidence](../results/R0.05b.md). R0.05a water placement is independently re-reviewed; its human playtest remains open. This brief does not authorize a timetable/perk draft, Tier 2 or R0.06.

## Bounded outcome

Make durable role-specific staff capacity a hiring opportunity, not free labour. In preparation, the player can hire an identifiable extra medic and/or steward into paid, weekend-only contracts, inspect/select each on the farm and assign independent physical jobs. Individual walking speed, steward calming/confrontation skill and medic treatment speed materially affect reachable, visible incident outcomes. Basic water, first aid, emergency isolation and steward response remain guaranteed counters; a weak or absent optional hire must never turn an otherwise eligible incident into an unavoidable death.

## Existing baseline and authorized prototype values

- Current Tier 1 has 20 mandatory guests, three performers, Casey Vale as the rostered sound worker, Riley Hart as the baseline medic and Jordan Hale as the baseline steward; optional Morgan Finch is a paid £15 maintenance worker. The existing Casey sound-shift contract costs £20 or £40 per weekend and one is required before opening. Riley and Jordan already travel physically. Current personal movement uses a deterministic 850–1150 permille pattern; baseline medical treatment is 480 ticks, and steward calming/confrontation actions are 240/160 ticks. These are starting measurements, not approval for new balance.
- **Proposed bounded foundation controls:** `EXTRA STEWARD SLOT`, `EXTRA MEDIC SLOT` and `ALL STEWARDS/MEDICS UPGRADED` would be durable role-specific effect seams, initially at most one additional slot per role. They would be labelled free demonstration controls until the separate perk draft defines entitlement, prices and draft timing; no £100 slot purchases, £80 individual training or cash effect is proposed for them. A slot is permission to hire, not a worker. An optional medic or steward contract is proposed at **£30 per weekend** as prototype tuning. Keep every guest, performer and worker within the 50-active-person boundary: with optional Morgan, Tier 2's present 40 guests + three performers + Casey/Riley/Jordan/Morgan = 47, leaving at most three headroom; two optional workers fit under 50, but Tier 2 is not implemented here.
- Baseline Riley/Jordan remain available without an extra slot or optional contract, and emergency isolation remains immediate. A role-specific slot and role-wide upgrade persist; known staff profiles persist. Every paid optional work contract expires at any weekend outcome, including Favour retry. Rehire consumes cash again; no slot, contact or upgrade creates a free active worker or an extra Favour/reward. Existing Casey/Morgan contract economics remain provisional and unchanged in this slice.

## Authorized implementation boundary

1. Add a small authoritative roster of stable staff profiles and durable role-specific slot/role-wide upgrade state. The three free foundation controls are preparation-only, once each, and save/hash with durable retry behavior. Optional £30 contracts are seed-stable, visibly disclose role, individual speed/skill, contract duration and cost, and reject unaffordable, duplicate, absent-slot, over-capacity or >50-active-person commitments atomically. Preserve existing Casey/Morgan contracts and Riley/Jordan baseline identities without rewriting earlier saves into free optional contracts.
2. Represent each hired person as a protected, physical agent with arrival/departure and one owned job at a time. Permit at least two independently assigned simultaneous jobs when two eligible workers exist; selection/inspector identifies the worker, target, route, queue/busy state and ETA. No teleport, double booking, invisible cancellation or generic unbounded job scheduler. Keep maintenance, medic and steward actions distinct, using existing incident causal histories and safe fallback controls.
3. Apply personal walking speed in the existing 850–1150 permille band to actual route progress and ETA; use the existing steward skill scale and make medic treatment time individual and bounded (prototype candidates **360/480/600 ticks**). The role-wide upgrade applies a simple clamped skill/speed modifier to all stewards/medics, not individual purchased training. Variation must be deterministic, saved and explainable in UI. Completed timely treatment remains dependable, but no upgrade guarantees rescue after the existing deadline. Multiple injuries/jobs show honest backlog and actionable warnings.
4. Reuse approved bodies, medic presentation and steward yoke accessory, with restrained colour/cue/name variation for identity. No new 3D asset is assumed. If a new asset proves essential, stop at the R0 asset gate and obtain exact coordinator and user approval before creating/integrating it.
5. Hash and save all capacity, profile, role-wide upgrade, contract, assignment, job and response timing state. Same-tier retry retains durable slots/upgrade/contact knowledge but expires contracts and resets attempt-scoped jobs/incident work. Reload must not reroll staff or duplicate hires, dispatches or ledger charges.

## Acceptance before any following brief

- Deterministic tests show activating a free role slot adds zero workers until a £30 paid hire; role-wide upgrade charges nothing in this foundation; unaffordable/duplicate/absent-slot/over-cap/50-boundary commands leave state unchanged. Tier 1 can still open and safely counter each existing eligible equipment, medical and disorder chain with only baseline coverage.
- Same-seed comparisons prove two independently travelling workers can own two different jobs; one person cannot own two jobs; slower/faster arrival, weaker/stronger steward skills and medic treatment duration have observable, bounded effects. Unreachable or busy responses report a specific reason, and timely baseline interventions still prevent attributable death.
- Save/reload mid-route, mid-job, mid-treatment and mid-disorder response preserves identity, ownership, deterministic result and hash. Failed-weekend/Favour retry and safe settlement expire contracts while retaining durable capacity, role-wide upgrade and known contacts without free work or repeated payments.
- Demonstrate preparation hire/selection and two simultaneous physical responses in a labelled rendered fixture; run focused chain/save tests, a proportionate <=50-active-person timing check and an isolated export/startup smoke. Obtain targeted independent review of transitions, persistence, ledger and safety, then record actual evidence in a new result file and `briefs/R0/PROGRESS.md`. Stop before timetable, perk draft, Tier 2 or R0.06.

## Approved outcome and boundaries

All attendees are adults, gamewide. One existing rounded foodvan and one visibly distinct drinks stall; products only chips, softdrink and beer. Free water remains the safety counter. Hunger, existing thirst and fictional normalized intoxication are separate. Stable tastes include abstainers. Decisions weigh needs, prices, physical travel/wait and current/upcoming music; no forced synchronized purchases. Physical FIFO/service purchases create a held item, then release the counter immediately and consume over time while watching/wandering. Money/stock/ownership settle once and survive save/reload. Beer can improve enjoyment, is not the best hydration; gradual intoxication can increase aggression by temperament and mildly reduce coordination. Visible bar/tipsy remarks/subtle high-level stagger, time recovery, food slowing uptake and water addressing thirst without instant sobriety. Clear severe warning precedes collapse; existing physical medic/deadline machinery handles it, no random death.

No dependency/addiction simulation, drink varieties, price editor, elaborate bartender, extra crowd kernel, Tier2/perk progression, new music, Mureka access or commit. No clinical alcohol/BAC claim. Futurethree-day15minweekend is intent; this slice targets one300s day at unchanged80ticks/sec.

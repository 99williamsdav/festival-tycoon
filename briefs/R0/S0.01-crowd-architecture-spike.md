## Stage 1 — movement architecture

Separate local avoidance from global path search. A legal sidestep should preserve the person's intended corridor/waypoint and rebase local movement without synchronously running full-grid A*. Replan globally on a real topology/destination change or after an explicitly bounded, deterministic stuck/invalid-route condition. Preserve swept-wall and corner checks, stable same-tick ordering, variable walking speeds, exact service arrival, physical queue reservations and no teleportation. Any new progress/replan counters that affect decisions are authoritative, hashed and saved. Measure how many global searches remain attributable to avoidance; do not infer success solely from fewer searches if blocked age, completion or correctness regresses.

Run focused navigation/queue/save tests and the existing S0.00 100-person representative and congested windows after this stage. Record a stage-specific build identity, exact commands, timings, searches, blocked-age and correctness against the unchanged baseline. If movement cannot improve the tail without a functional regression after one focused repair pass, stop and report the measured tradeoff before changing snapshot publication.

## Stage 2 — observation and snapshot architecture

Remove full snapshot construction and SHA-256 hashing from every authoritative tick when the caller only needs progression or presentation positions. Keep a complete `CaptureSnapshot` and canonical hash at explicit test, save and verification boundaries. Ensure the production scheduler and diagnostic runner use a bounded observation path that preserves every person's identity and position; no authoritative decision may depend on whether a camera or presentation consumer is attached. Document when snapshots/hashes are produced, maintain exact save/restore and same-build deterministic continuation, and report allocation/GC as well as timing changes.

Re-run the same 100-person windows and, if correct, bounded 200-person representative/congested headless probes. Keep timing components non-overlapping and source-identifiable. Do not weaken the correctness checks to obtain a faster result. A rendered 50/100/200 gate is a separate follow-up if headless results justify it; no rendered success may be inferred from headless work.

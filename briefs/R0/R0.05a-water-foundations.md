# R0.05a — bounded water foundations

Status: **Implemented; independent review passed; human playtest/acceptance pending.** This is a foundation inserted after accepted R0.05 and before the deferred R0.06/Tier 2 gate. Do not start staff slots, timetable, perk draft, or R0.06 in this brief.

## Product decisions

- Keep the existing Hot-scenario primary standpipe. During preparation, allow at most two additional standpipes at fixed, individually identified, suitable sites. Placement changes the world asset, pick target, route, queue, and service point together. Reuse the approved standpipe asset. No tower proxy is approved.
- Each tap has one independent physical-arrival FIFO, ten queue places, a visible overflow tail, and at most one drinker. Approaching a line reserves nothing. A guest chooses once, using distance and estimated wait with deterministic tie-breaking; the choice remains stable while seeking or queued, except when the chosen line becomes unavailable. Water complaints follow the person's actual line.
- More taps **never divide pressure or reduce any person's drinking rate**. There is no shared-pressure, pipe, or flow network. Ordinary personal relief remains 8/12/16/20 thirst units per tick. Council sharing first caps the personal baseline at **12**, then a tower adds **4**. Thus tower alone gives 12/16/20/24 and tower with sharing gives 12/16/16/16. Heat relief is one quarter of final thirst relief. These values are prototype tuning, not a final balance claim.
- Keep point identity/layout and tower effect in authoritative saved state and hash. Added standpipes and tower are durable properties on a same-tier retry; a one-weekend sharing commitment remains attempt-scoped. A narrow saved-effect seam applies the extra-tap and tower effects as free preparation choices for this foundation demonstration only; it is not the later random draft, price, or entitlement design.
- The exact [water tower prototype v1](../../art/reviews/R0.05a/ASSET_APPROVALS.md) was approved during implementation and integrated at the farmhouse-east site with a 3.5 × 3.5 m navigation reservation. This approval does not extend to any other asset.
- Keep the existing 50-active-person boundary. Label all deterministic setup/capture scenarios as fixtures. Do not claim a larger crowd capacity from this work.

## Acceptance evidence

Prove two simultaneous drinkers, unchanged per-person rate when another tap is added, slow-drinker and tower/sharing compositions, stable sensible choice, physical-arrival FIFO, point-correct overflow/disorder, save/restore and durable retry, and player-visible placement/picking. Run focused tests, a bounded rendered demonstration, an isolated export/startup check, and a targeted independent review. Record actual results in `briefs/results/R0.05a.md` and `briefs/R0/PROGRESS.md`, then stop before the next brief.

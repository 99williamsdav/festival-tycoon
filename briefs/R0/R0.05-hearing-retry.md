## Approved R0.05 product decisions — 25 September 2026

- Start the campaign with exactly one Council Favour. First real death freezes the edition, records one casualty and one hearing exactly once. Spend one Favour for a same-tier retry; with none, the campaign is lost. No safe closure/evacuation option: **the show must go on**. No council safety loan or cash-purchased Favour.
- Every authorized retry resets cash and consumable stock to that tier's defined starting baselines, not the failed weekend's remainder. For the currently implemented two-tier preparation model these are `CampaignDefaults.OpeningCashPennies` = £800 and the initial stock quantity = 40 units for both tiers; R0.06 may separately authorize different later-tier baselines. Unused purchased stock from the failed weekend does **not** persist. Owned durable property does persist; rentals and work contracts expire; tier seed and revealed offers remain stable.
- A once-per-campaign community choice shares free-water capacity with a neighbouring community for one edition. The tap caps an individual's drinking relief at **12 thirst units/tick** while active, versus ordinary personal rates of 8, 12, 16 or 20: natural 8/12 drinkers are unchanged, while 16/20 drinkers are capped. The sacrifice, estimated queue effect, and exact cap must be shown before commitment and at the tap during play. Award one Favour only when the committed sharing obligation is actually honoured, with one stable claim ID; no cash purchase, repeated claim or reload farming.
- **Resolved anti-exploit rule:** unused purchased stock resets with all consumables to the starting 40 units on every authorized retry. Repeated early deaths cannot accumulate purchased stock while cash is restored. The durable sound rig remains a one-time owned purchase rather than a stackable or saleable good.

## Approved player-facing copy and visual gate

- Always call editions **“weekends”** in player-facing R0.05 text. `Edition` may remain an internal type/identifier; do not expose it in new player copy.
- Approved v4 shows the heading **“Council hearing”** and lead exactly **“Someone has died. We explicitly told you this shouldn’t happen. We’re considering revoking your licence.”** The Favour spend/retry choice is exactly **“It won’t happen again”** and makes the consequence clear beside the choice: it spends **1 Council Favour** and retries the **same tier's weekend**. It includes a post-death **Concede / End campaign** choice, distinct from a pre-death safe closure. Keep its approved visual text intact in the Godot integration.

## Scope

- Connect the first real incident death to frozen settlement, casualty history and hearing exactly once. Apply approved cash/stock/rental/contract persistence.
- Spend one real Favour to create a same-tier retry. Restore the approved tier starting cash and stock baselines exactly once; do not add a council loan.
- Implement one edition of shared water capacity, the 12/tick cap for otherwise faster drinkers, precise pre-commitment and live tap disclosure, and a stable once-per-campaign Favour claim awarded only after the obligation is honoured.

Excludes success rewards, tier advance, score and endless.

## Acceptance, demonstration and stop

- With one Favour: death → one hearing → one debit → viable same-tier retry. With zero: coherent campaign loss. Reload/duplicate commands cannot repeat casualty, cost, Favour, package or claim.
- At least two sequential authorized retries remain counter-viable under worst approved carryover, without accumulating value. Deliberate early death never increases saleable wealth/options.
- Community choice exposes its exact rate cap before acceptance and during play, applies only to the committed edition, and awards after actual honouring once. No safe-closure action is present.

Demonstrate real death/hearing/retry through normal UI after the commissioned UI asset is explicitly approved; until then use clearly non-final debug interaction only if needed for backend testing. Demonstrate zero-Favour loss and the one-edition water trade-off; run focused economy/save abuse cases and review exactly-once transactions. Stop before success progression.

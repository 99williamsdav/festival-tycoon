# R0.02 — equipment causal chain

Status: **Functionally reviewed; performance gate failed, stopped for user decision.** Outcome: one equipment fault is readable, preventable and terminal only after ignored escalation. See [implementation and bounded-check evidence](../results/R0.02.md).

## Approved start direction (23 September 2026)

- The temporary power unit is a compact towable generator with an integrated breaker panel, supplying the trailer stage's sound and lights. The sound rig consumes power; it is not the generator. Fuel and cable-network simulation are out of scope.
- Load and condition are visible before trouble. A named overload alert gives at least **45 real seconds** before lethal escalation, with reachable response routes and additional margin if measured travel requires it. Values remain tunable after playtesting; no calm-to-death jump.
- Shed load and emergency isolation are available immediately through the unit's controls. Maintenance is performed by a physically travelling worker with an actual job delay, never by instant remote repair. A fault can kill only a protected person actually nearby, with deterministic causal attribution.
- Demonstrate prevention in the normal route and ignored escalation in a clearly labelled fixture through the real lifecycle. Full playable hearing/retry economy remains in R0.05.
- First asset concept: one slightly rustic low-poly generator with readable normal, overloaded, isolated and fault states plus a clear cutoff switch. Exact v1 approved by coordinator and user on 23 September 2026 and committed as `940fe3a`; see `art/reviews/R0.02/ASSET_APPROVALS.md`. This approval does not authorize game integration or the remaining maintenance/exclusion cues.
- Approved 23 September 2026: initially place the generator at one stage-side spot, with its position authoritative and persisted so later movement can be added without treating it as immovable property. No placement UI or cable routing is in this brief.
- Approved 23 September 2026: guarantee an affordable maintenance-worker offer before the equipment hazard can be eligible. Hiring the worker creates a paid edition contract and a protected physical worker who must travel to the unit for repair. If the player declines that offer, the emergency cutoff remains a safe intervention.
- Approved 23 September 2026: use code-native paper/colour role cues for the worker and a clear code-native exclusion/action marker. No second bespoke 3D asset is needed for this prototype.

The user has provisionally accepted S0.08's occasional performance hitches to progress, without treating them as fixed or as a capacity pass. Keep proportional runtime checks as incident behavior is added.

## Scope

Implement only overload/condition → visible evidence → overload warning → shed load, isolate or maintenance response → dangerous fault → attributable death. Baseline isolation and maintenance access must predate eligibility. Response includes real delay/ownership; no magical remote repair. Persist trigger evidence, warning acknowledgement, intervention/job, escalation state and causal event IDs.

## Acceptance, demonstration and stop

- Controlled seeds prove each timely intervention prevents death; insufficient/late action explains why it failed. Calm state cannot jump to death and the warning window meets the approved minimum.
- Closing/isolation releases affected reservations safely; cash/stock/job changes are atomic. Save at every stage resumes identically with no duplicate alert, job, casualty or charge.
- Terminal attribution names person/role, load/condition, warning and response. No clinical or graphic detail.

Demonstrate one prevented normal route and one labelled fixture escalation through the real lifecycle. Run focused chain/save tests and review causal history plus asset readability. Stop; do not start medical/disorder content.

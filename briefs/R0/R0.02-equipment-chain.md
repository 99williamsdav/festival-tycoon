## Scope

Implement only overload/condition → visible evidence → overload warning → shed load, isolate or maintenance response → dangerous fault → attributable death. Baseline isolation and maintenance access must predate eligibility. Response includes real delay/ownership; no magical remote repair. Persist trigger evidence, warning acknowledgement, intervention/job, escalation state and causal event IDs.

## Acceptance, demonstration and stop

- Controlled seeds prove each timely intervention prevents death; insufficient/late action explains why it failed. Calm state cannot jump to death and the warning window meets the approved minimum.
- Closing/isolation releases affected reservations safely; cash/stock/job changes are atomic. Save at every stage resumes identically with no duplicate alert, job, casualty or charge.
- Terminal attribution names person/role, load/condition, warning and response. No clinical or graphic detail.

Demonstrate one prevented normal route and one labelled fixture escalation through the real lifecycle. Run focused chain/save tests and review causal history plus asset readability. Stop; do not start medical/disorder content.

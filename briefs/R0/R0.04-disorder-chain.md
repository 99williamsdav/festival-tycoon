# R0.04 — disorder causal chain

Status: **Gameplay direction and security-post concept approved; model and other asset checkpoints pending.** Outcome: visible service/crowd frustration can be de-escalated instead of becoming a fatal disorder chain.

## Read and dependency

Read active design incident contract; `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` §§4, 6, 9–14; R0.01 music consequence and the equipment/medical implementations; and the R0 asset audit. Dependency: accepted R0.03 and approved exact pressure thresholds/interventions. Approve security post/cue and abstract state assets/proxies at brief start.

## Scope

Implement a shared disgruntlement/aggression system, proved by both existing scenarios: (1) cutting off a performance that people care about and (2) excessive waiting for water while thirsty. Each person has a stable hidden temperament affecting how quickly they anger; band enthusiasm, thirst, personal queue tolerance and actual grievance duration determine pressure. A complaint (for example, “Hurry up!”), visible agitation and argument precede any fight. Hidden traits vary reactions but cannot cause an untelegraphed attack.

The grievance can be resolved (music restored, queue relieved) and some arguments subside on their own. Otherwise dispatch a physically travelling security worker. Security has distinct variable calming and confrontation skills: an unsuccessful calming attempt or late arrival can redirect aggression toward the worker; a worker who loses a confrontation can be incapacitated and require the same medical response as other protected people. Security is not an automatic cancel button. Preserve participant, location, contributing events, security and medical timing. Do not assign misconduct from genre.

Use abstract but unmistakable confrontation/injury cues; fight animation is not required for prototype acceptance. No graphic violence. Implement both music and queue trigger/prevention routes end-to-end, not a scripted mandatory incident. Retain safe area/service closure and egress as an intervention.

## Acceptance, demonstration and stop

- Remove pressure/provocation and eligibility disappears. Demonstrate both music and queue prevention, spontaneous diffusion, timely security de-escalation, failed/late security response, and medical treatment of any injured participant including security. Each approved timely intervention changes the chain; closing an area preserves egress and cannot itself create an unavoidable death.
- Security/medical agents travel or show honest backlog; they do not teleport. Alerts deduplicate. Save/reload across argument, dispatch, injury and treatment is exact.
- Seed sweep shows well-served/countered states materially reduce escalation and no mandatory drama quota forces incidents.

Demonstrate prevented/de-escalated normal routes and one labelled fixture terminal route. Focus review on causal links, egress, response ownership and social-content boundaries. Stop before real hearing economy.

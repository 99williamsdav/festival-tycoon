## Scope

- Add the smallest campaign/edition outcome state needed for tier ID, attempt ID/status, protected person identity/role, casualty record, hearing, Favour balance and outcome transaction IDs.
- Use a tiny development fixture with one explicitly forced death, one fixture Favour, one same-tier retry, then an explicitly forced safe outcome and tier advance. Forced outcomes must be unmistakably fixture-only and unreachable in normal UI.
- Freeze on the first death; append one casualty and failed attempt; create one hearing; spend one fixture Favour once; create one retry; settle one safe attempt and advance once.
- Hash and persist every authoritative field. Latest autosave resumes the terminal/hearing/success boundary; older manual saves remain loadable. Reprocessing any restored boundary is idempotent.

Excludes real roster sizes/time, offers, cash/stock carryover, incident eligibility/counters, user-facing debrief, real rewards, safe closure and art.

## Acceptance, demonstration and stop

- Same commands/seed yield the same IDs/hash. Two deaths in one tick produce exactly one terminal outcome; later commands/ticks cannot mutate the frozen edition.
- Save/reload immediately before death, after death, at hearing, after spend/retry and after safe advance matches uninterrupted state. Duplicate spend, settlement, casualty and tier advance reject without mutation.
- A guest, staff and performer fixture can each be the terminal subject; unknown/unprotected subjects reject.
- Focused tests cover injected save failure before every boundary: prior valid state remains and no half-transition appears.

Demonstrate the headless fixture's full failure → spend/retry → safe/advance trace with IDs and hashes. Record focused build/tests and a review of transition/persistence code. Stop; do not create gameplay hazards, screens, assets or claim playability.

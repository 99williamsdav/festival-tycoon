# R0.01 — preparation, roster and persistent farm

Status: **Accepted bounded implementation; runtime/pacing limitations recorded in the result.** Outcome: prepare one real fixed-roster edition from bounded meaningful choices while the farm survives retries.

## Approved start decisions (23 September 2026)

- Tier 1 has 20 mandatory attendees and Tier 2 has 40; all on-site guests, staff and performers together remain at or below 50 active people. This is a prototype boundary, not a population-capacity claim.
- Target approximately 10 real minutes for a complete early-tier attempt, including ordinary preparation and pauses. Tune exact Friday–Sunday lengths from playtesting without silently changing the authoritative clock conversion.
- Purchased equipment and permanent works persist. Rentals and individual staff work contracts expire after each edition; known staff contacts persist.
- Failed editions retain current cash, owned property and unused nonperishable stock after actual consumption and committed costs; give no post-death revenue or reward. The retry retains its tier seed and revealed offers.
- Simple outfit/accessory role cues and code-native paper badges are approved as R0.01 prototypes; no new bespoke art is required at this gate.
- Offer implementation may choose the smallest deterministic bounded set satisfying the brief's material-choice and essential-counter tests. Exact offer contents, prices and economy viability must be evidenced in review rather than treated as a new product rule.

P3 crossing-liveness proof is parked, **not accepted**. This brief may use the existing small-crowd production movement, but must not integrate the isolated traffic kernel or claim 200-person support. Escalate if the roster cannot meet the 50-active-person boundary or playable movement cannot complete.

- The mandatory roster cannot be reduced/refused; arrivals cannot double-admit and staff/performers are neither teleported nor exempted under the approved representation.
- Same seed offers the same bounded choices; at least two choices materially change cost/counter/quality evidence. Essential counters remain obtainable across a seed sweep.
- Success/failure save fixtures preserve ownership, rentals, contacts, contracts, cash and stock according to the approved matrix; no negative cash, duplicate item or free contract.
- A weak-fit act measurably worsens actual guest satisfaction/risk input under a controlled identical roster, while survival eligibility remains separate.

Demonstrate preparation, two meaningful choices, fixed-roster start and save/reload through normal UI, plus a focused headless comparison. Stop before any chain can kill. Remeasure only if active total/behaviour materially changes M1.00's representative load; otherwise explicitly defer to R0.06.

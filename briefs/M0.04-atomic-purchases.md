# M0.04 — Atomic cash purchase and stock accounting

Status at authoring: Not started. Milestone: M0 foundation.

## Assignment

One guest can buy one item from a festival-owned service without creating money, negative stock or duplicate purchases.

Dependencies: M0.03 accepted.

Read [shared execution rules](README.md#shared-execution-rules), then these targeted sections of the repository-root documents: TECHNICAL_SPEC.md §§3, 8; GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md §6; CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md §2. Read additional sections only when needed to resolve a concrete dependency.

## Scope

- Add integer-penny wallets, festival cash, owned stock/cost basis and an immutable transaction record with explicit balanced account entries.
- Implement a minimal purchase operation through the command path. Queue/service timing will call the same operation in M0.08.
- Validate funds, stock, positive price/quantity and identities before committing any state.
- Persist transaction identities in authoritative state for later save work.
- Use a small exact-value fixture; the full annual loan/settlement model is deferred.

## Acceptance checks

1. Guest starts with 500p; purchase at 300p leaves 200p, increases festival cash by 300p and consumes one stock unit.
2. Stock cost and sales revenue remain distinct; ledger entries balance.
3. Exact-cash purchase succeeds with zero remaining cash; insufficient cash and empty stock reject without any mutation.
4. Repeating the same accepted transaction/command cannot charge twice.
5. Two buyers competing for the last item produce exactly one successful sale in deterministic submission order.
6. Negative quantities/prices and missing owners are rejected; cumulative cash transfers reconcile.

These are required outcomes, not a prescribed test implementation. Add meaningful automated checks where feasible and report manual observations honestly.

## Exclusions

No ATM/card, vendor revenue share, loans, taxes, finance screen or yearly accounting dashboard.

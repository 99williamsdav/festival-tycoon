# M0.08 — Small physical queue completes purchases

Status at authoring: Not started. Milestone: M0 foundation.

## Assignment

Five agents physically queue, receive timed service and pay exactly once, with reservations cleaned up on departure or closure.

Dependencies: M0.07 accepted.

Read [shared execution rules](README.md#shared-execution-rules), then these targeted sections of the repository-root documents: TECHNICAL_SPEC.md §§6, 8, 11; GAME_DESIGN_SPEC.md §12; CONTENT_AND_BALANCE.md §4. Read additional sections only when needed to resolve a concrete dependency.

## Scope

- Add an ordered queue and one service slot, with stable arrival/submission tie-breaking and separate physical queue positions.
- Route guests to reserved slots; service begins only when the front guest actually reaches the service position.
- Finish the configured service duration by calling M0.04's purchase operation once.
- Handle insufficient cash, stockout, agent abandonment and facility closure. For this small task, closure cancels unfinished service without payment and releases all reservations.
- Add minimal separation for these sparse agents; general dense movement is M0.09.
- Extend snapshots for queues, active slot owner, remaining service duration and agent intentions.

## Acceptance checks

1. Five eligible guests are served in defined order; money/stock reconcile to five sales.
2. A guest delayed en route cannot receive remote service/payment.
3. Insufficient funds or stock releases the front slot and allows the queue to resolve.
4. Abandonment/closure releases every affected reservation; reopening does not resurrect stale members.
5. Save mid-service and at completion boundary; resumed state/transactions match uninterrupted execution.
6. Exactly one slot owner exists at a time; repeated completion handling cannot charge twice.

These are required outcomes, not a prescribed test implementation. Add meaningful automated checks where feasible and report manual observations honestly.

## Exclusions

No full needs-based AI, staffing departments, multiple tills, ATM, vendors or queue satisfaction.

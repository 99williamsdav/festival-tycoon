# M0.09 — Fifty-agent foundation scene and time controls

Implementation status: **Implemented — independent review pending**. Implementation completed locally on 15 September 2026.

## Delivered foundation behaviour

The default exported scene now runs fifty simulation-owned attendees from multiple deterministic gate-area spawn positions into one physical queue. All fifty use the approved shared 236-triangle attendee mesh and ten approved presentation-only palettes, assigned as stable fixture ordinal modulo ten (five attendees per palette). Palette identity does not enter authoritative state or hashes. The intentions are development-fixture attendee AI; there are no player controls for attendee destinations or queue membership.

The queue has fifty distinct half-metre-cell reservations in a single readable line, one service owner and fifty distinct in-field exits. Stock and wallets allow all guests to buy the existing generic 300p item. A stable spatial neighbour index supports deterministic nearby queries. Navigation proposes fixed-tick movement from prior occupancy and resolves coincident moving proposals by remaining-corridor progress then stable ID. Rejected proposals retain their exact prior movement state. This is bounded prototype separation with compressed crowds, not full body physics or crowd-safety simulation; distinct queue/service/exit reservations remain the hard ownership guarantee.

The Godot presentation uses a fixed-tick accumulator for Pause/1×/2×/4×. At most 16 ticks are processed per rendered frame and debt is bounded to 320 ticks. Overload reduces attained speed and raises a visible warning; it never advances a partial/skipped authoritative event. Pause freezes navigation and service while the camera, inspection and Save/Load UI continue to run.

Manual Save and Load use the accepted M0.05 atomic gzip-JSON path in a dedicated `manual-foundation` slot. Autosave runs every 800 ticks (200 festival seconds; 50 real seconds at 1×), maps cadence index modulo three to `autosave-0..2`, and uses the same validated temporary-write/atomic-replace/last-good-backup path. Loading recovery selects the highest-tick valid rotation member and ignores a corrupt newer member. Manual and autosave slots cannot collide. Queue order, owner, remaining service time, movement and transactions were already authoritative and persisted; the neighbour index and palettes are derived/cosmetic and add no hidden save state.

## Limitations

- This is the M0 foundation/stress scene, not a playable festival. Intentions, stock and service duration are fixture configuration; needs, preferences, programmes, staff and multiple destinations remain later work.
- The separation layer prevents coincident moving proposals and persistent stacking/deadlock but deliberately permits strong visual compression. The 15 mm sampled minimum is honest evidence that this is not finished body-radius or crowd-safety behaviour.
- Attendees still have no rig or walking animation. The ten variants are material colourways on one mesh, not clothing/body geometry variants.
- The benchmark is one local development laptop and is not a supported-hardware claim. Subjective control feel and atmosphere were not manually playtested.
- Nothing was pushed and M0.10 was not started.

# M0.09 — Fifty-agent foundation scene and time controls

Implementation status: **Accepted**. Implementation completed locally on 15 September 2026; final independent review accepted the reviewed implementation HEAD.

## Delivered foundation behaviour

The default exported scene now runs fifty simulation-owned attendees from multiple deterministic gate-area spawn positions into one physical queue. All fifty use the approved shared 236-triangle attendee mesh and ten approved presentation-only palettes, assigned as stable fixture ordinal modulo ten (five attendees per palette). Palette identity does not enter authoritative state or hashes. The intentions are development-fixture attendee AI; there are no player controls for attendee destinations or queue membership.

The queue has fifty distinct half-metre-cell reservations in a single readable line, one service owner and fifty distinct in-field exits. Stock and wallets allow all guests to buy the existing generic 300p item. A stable spatial neighbour index supports deterministic nearby queries. Navigation proposes fixed-tick movement from prior occupancy and resolves proposals against stationary and already accepted occupancy by remaining-corridor progress then stable ID. Deterministic lateral yielding targets 300 mm centre clearance without changing route progress; rejected proposals retain their exact prior movement state. Every ordinary and avoidance segment is validated by an exact integer supercover against the traversal grid, conservatively including boundary/corner contact and the M0.07 no-diagonal-corner-cut rule; illegal return-to-route shortcuts yield or choose another deterministic offset. Presentation interpolates only between these legal consecutive authoritative endpoints. This is bounded prototype separation, not full body physics or crowd-safety simulation; distinct queue/service/exit reservations remain the hard ownership guarantee.

The Godot presentation uses a fixed-tick accumulator for Pause/1×/2×/4×. At most 16 ticks are processed per rendered frame and debt is bounded to 320 ticks. Each scheduled tick is advanced and snapshotted individually, so presentation interpolation always spans the final two consecutive authoritative ticks—even across multi-tick 4× frames and route turns—and resets on pause/load/discontinuity. Overload reduces attained speed and raises a visible warning; it never advances a partial/skipped authoritative event. Pause freezes navigation and service while the camera, inspection and Save/Load UI continue to run. Diagnostics distinguish queue membership from physical waiting: `Waiting` requires an arrived attendee at their assigned slot and excludes travellers and the active service owner.

Manual Save and Load use the accepted M0.05 atomic gzip-JSON path in a dedicated `manual-foundation` slot. Production autosave uses presentation-side monotonic elapsed time and writes once every 300 real seconds, independent of simulation speed, pause and overload. A delayed frame schedules at most one safe-boundary write, preventing bursts; slots rotate by a successful autosave generation persisted in the save header. On restart the next generation is recovered from valid slots, and newest recovery follows that chronology even after a manual rollback to a lower simulation tick. A corrupt newest generation falls back to the prior valid generation. Existing slots without sequence metadata remain compatible: sequenced slots supersede legacy slots, while an all-legacy set falls back to persisted UTC timestamp then descending slot path for deterministic equal-time ties. The exported evidence route explicitly overrides the cadence to two real seconds so rotation can be exercised in a bounded capture. Manual and autosave slots cannot collide. Manual load rebases elapsed autosave time and clears clock debt, achieved-speed samples and interpolation history, regardless of whether the loaded tick is forward or backward. Queue order, owner, remaining service time, movement and transactions remain authoritative and persisted; the neighbour index, interpolation and palettes are derived/cosmetic and add no hidden save state.

## Limitations

- This is the M0 foundation/stress scene, not a playable festival. Intentions, stock and service duration are fixture configuration; needs, preferences, programmes, staff and multiple destinations remain later work.
- The separation layer prevents exact coincident positions, sustained near-superposition, discrete or swept blocked-cell crossings, diagonal corner cuts and persistent stacking/deadlock in the full every-tick 50-agent fixture. Its measured **299 mm** minimum is a prototype centre-clearance result, not body physics or a crowd-safety guarantee.
- Attendees still have no rig or walking animation. The ten variants are material colourways on one mesh, not clothing/body geometry variants.
- The benchmark is one local development laptop and is not a supported-hardware claim. Subjective control feel and atmosphere were not manually playtested.
- Nothing was pushed and M0.10 was not started.

The approved [corrective follow-up](../M0.09-corrective-follow-up.md) also brought forward the intended arrival-based queue semantics and walking-speed variation so M0.10 will benchmark the behavior intended to survive.

- WASD and arrow keys now map to screen up/down/left/right at all four rotations through one pure screen-to-world mapping; middle-drag, bounds, rotation, selection and pause behavior remain unchanged.
- The service slot begins 2.5 m west of the service-point centre, immediately outside its footprint. Fifty one-metre-spaced slots form a nearby five-row serpentine queue on validated walkable terrain, removing the disconnected gap without crossing building footprints.
- All foundation attendees begin as `ApproachingQueue`: they own no queue membership or slot. Physical arrival assigns admission tick and a separately persisted/hashed authoritative arrival sequence. Sorting uses tick, then arrival sequence, then stable ID only for a true tie; an active service owner remains first. The M0.08 fixture retains its accepted advance-enrolment setup solely for backward-compatible milestone regression.
- Foundation attendee speed follows a stable seven-value 850–1150 permille pattern. It is explicit authoritative state, persisted and hashed, and only changes navigation allowance—not service time. Save migration treats the absent/zero field as the former 1000-permille rate.

Movement now reserves every mover's prior position while proposals resolve, and a yielding offset rebases route interpolation at its accepted authoritative position. Arrivals may never be offset: they must occupy the exact destination. This prevents both mover swaps and a service owner being declared present away from the counter.

### Independent-review repair

Independent review found that the public rejoin command could still append a physical-arrival queue member remotely and reuse command submission sequence instead of the queue-owned arrival counter. The repair persists/hashes an explicit physical-admission mode per queue. In that mode, every initial admission and rejoin allocates only from `NextArrivalSequence`, while a rejoin command merely transfers the attendee back to a coherent `ai.service-approach` journey with no membership or reserved slot. The active owner remains protected. The legacy M0.08 fixture keeps its command-sequence behavior behind the explicit non-physical fixture mode and cannot leak into the foundation path.

Validation now rejects duplicate or stale allocated sequences, preserves a strictly-greater next counter across save/load, and fails an arrival closed if the unsigned counter is exhausted rather than wrapping. Focused regressions reproduce attendee 16's abandon/rejoin after automatic admission and attendee 4's first-admission/close/reopen/rejoin flow; both remain remote and unreserved during approach, allocate a fresh unique sequence only on exact physical arrival, and restore successfully. Malformed duplicate and stale persisted states are rejected, and the exhaustion boundary is covered. Camera tests now assert the exact expected diagonal world vector for screen Up and Right at each rotation, so the former map-fixed orientation-zero implementation fails the test.

Final persistence review identified that saves written before the explicit queue-mode field could deserialize it as legacy `false`. The DTO is now presence-aware: explicit `true` or `false` is preserved, while a genuinely absent field is inferred as physical only from bounded old-M0.09 evidence—an advanced queue arrival counter, an approaching attendee, or a persisted non-default foundation walking speed. This preserves explicit M0.08 legacy fixtures without inventing a broad migration. Null remains omitted during checksum reconstruction, so old serialized payload checksums remain stable. Canonical queue hashing advances to schema 4; restore additionally accepts the two exact prior queue hash shapes (schema 3 without the field and schema 3 with it), after which subsequent saves normalize to schema 4. A JSON regression removes the field genuinely, round-trips it through serialization, verifies checksum stability, restores the physical foundation semantics, and proves close/reopen rejoin stays unreserved until arrival; a separately serialized explicit-false M0.08 fixture remains legacy.

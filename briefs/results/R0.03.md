## Outcome and causal contract

A fixed Hot Tier 1 scenario begins with one free water point and one admitted baseline medic, Riley Hart. Twenty protected guests have deterministic thirst/heat state; eight begin moderately thirsty and Guest 20 starts at elevated risk. Each admitted guest weighs thirst and heat urgency, travel, physical queue wait and band enthusiasm, with a 240-tick decision cooldown. The selected-person inspector exposes both needs, intent and reason. No paid-water economy or changing-weather model was introduced.

The free tap has ten owned physical waiting slots and a 560-tick (7-second at 80 ticks/s) refill, with no stock or payment. Queue abandonment releases and reassigns reservations; service only advances after physical arrival at the front. The target warns at thirst 9,000/10,000 and heat 8,000/10,000, collapses 1,600 ticks (20 seconds) later, becomes critical 800 ticks (10 seconds) after collapse, and dies 2,400 ticks (30 seconds) after collapse if still untreated. A dispatched medic must physically reach a walkable position beside the stopped guest and complete 480 ticks (6 seconds) of treatment. Water, rest, medic treatment or gate-reached safe removal prevent the death when timely. These are fictional prototype thresholds, not clinical assertions.

Commands reject wrong targets, an already-owned medic response and unreachable water/rest/gate/medic destinations with specific causes. Treatment releases the medical hold and returns the guest to a listening route. Safe removal only settles on physical gate arrival, marks the person departed, and excludes them from later listening rewards. A terminal death records one causal protected-person casualty and hearing, then freezes progression; the medical state, queue, routes, evidence and timing are hashed and saved. The accepted base export used save compatibility `0.0.1-r0.03-hot-medical-v5`. The follow-up uses `0.0.1-r0.03-hot-medical-v6` because refill ownership/intent and snapshot validation changed; v5 saves are not compatible with v6.

Review repairs: while medic travel/treatment or gate removal owns Guest 20, conflicting water/rest/queue/route commands are rejected before changing reservations. Generic navigation commands are already forbidden in every prepared edition. Treatment completion also rechecks physical guest–medic proximity; if separation occurs, ownership is released with an explicit interrupted-response event instead of completing remotely. The single prototype rest destination is explicitly restricted to the at-risk guest; ordinary guests can use free water rather than becoming stuck at a rest cell. The visible panel shows time to collapse/critical/death and elapsed treatment percentage/time remaining.

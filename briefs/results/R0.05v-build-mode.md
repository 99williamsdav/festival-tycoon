# R0.05v — fresh festival build mode

28 September 2026. Implemented the approved build-mode slice on the R0.05u/R0.05s foundations. No new models or workwear were commissioned: the already approved v3 maintenance/sound assets and existing service models are reused. The ordinary New Game route now starts with six movable services unplaced, while historical non-build fixtures retain their old defaults. Fixed farm, stage and generator remain unchanged. The board's explicit valid/invalid inset was not copied into runtime; the user-corrected ghost, X collapse and small rotate/cancel hints are used.

## Behaviour delivered

- Authoritative six-service catalogue, stable identities, per-kind limits and rotations; grass, footprint, queue/door, fixed-object and essential-route validation; paid draft fees from one simulation table. The £800 opening cash is unchanged. Standard layout is £300. Defaults require a confirmation showing replaced/new service counts, price and full-draft delta; non-layout bookings, staffing, equipment, stock and perks stay.
- The Build destination has native 3D thumbnails, names, fees, counts and keyboard-focusable actions, an editable placed list, move/remove controls, a Before opening checklist and a full draft/budget footer. Placement ghosts follow the cursor, appear normally at valid sites and red when blocked. Invalid clicks leave the draft intact and show a concrete reason. Esc cancels; comma/period rotate; Start is blocked while placing and its confirmation includes the complete charge.
- Service charges enter the one-time atomic Start setup payment and ledger. Unpaid removal changes only the draft; failed-edition retry carries the prior paid layout forward as an editable unpaid plan, without live queue, occupancy, tank or stock consumption. Current-version save/restore validates the layout against physical mirrors and includes it in authoritative hashing; unsupported old files are not migrated or deleted.
- Two toilets have separate physical queues, owners, visits, tanks, smell and deterministic nearest/queue-aware choice. Two taps likewise remain separate physical service points and no longer depend on the Another Round perk in build mode. Missing optional vendors and not-yet-placed mandatory services do not instantiate invisible defaults. The existing water controls and legacy fixture routes remain.

Changed areas: `src/Festival.Simulation/BuildLayout.cs`, `Preparation.cs`, `PreparationPlan.cs`, `GameSession.cs`, `Toilets.cs`, `Immersion.cs` and adjacent medical/queue/post/perk code; `src/Festival.Persistence/EquipmentCommandCoordinator.cs`; `game/Main.BuildMode.cs`, `Main.BuildCapture.cs` and related Main/HUD/world-render files; `tests/Festival.Tests/BuildLayoutTests.cs`; this brief, index and progress record. No assets, night/Tier 2/cleaning/perk additions.

## Demonstration route and limits

Launch the isolated EXE with its adjacent data folder and choose New Game. Open **Build**: the farm has no placed movable services, and the checklist says what prevents opening. Select a row to place; move the cursor over grass or an occupied site, rotate with comma/period, click to confirm or Esc to cancel. Use **Use defaults…** to inspect the six-service/£300 comparison and install the standard layout. Select a placed service to move/remove, then complete programme and sound staffing before Start. After an edition fails, retry to edit the carried layout. The scripted captures and tests exercise the corresponding deterministic states; no manual final-app walkthrough was performed by user direction. No general FPS or long-play feel certification is claimed; the existing ≤50-person bound and R0.05u save cadence are retained.

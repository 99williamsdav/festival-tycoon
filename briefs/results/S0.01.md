The simple trial kept the existing corridor and rebased interpolation after a legal sidestep. It met the provisional timing targets and removed timed-window avoidance searches, but reached tick 25,000 with only **40/100** purchases, a non-empty backlog and unreleased reservations. The repair added swept offset-to-next-waypoint validation and an authoritative, persisted and hashed 32-conflict counter before a global recovery search. It also met the timing targets, but reached tick 25,000 with only **38/100** purchases, a non-empty backlog and unreleased reservations. Both trials retained exact boundary save/restore hash identity and reported no route failures, but both failed final correctness and completion.

The evidence proves a bounded-completion regression under these two designs: the unchanged baseline completed this same-seed representative 100-person run by tick 22,314, whereas neither trial completed by tick 25,000. It does not prove permanent deadlock or one root cause. Plausible contributors include corridor rebasing under repeated local contention, the chosen recovery condition, and interaction with physical queue compaction, but distinguishing them requires a separately approved architecture investigation. Lower search count and faster timed windows are not accepted as success while people remain unresolved.

## Decision options

1. Prototype a bounded local corridor repair that searches only a small deterministic neighbourhood and rejoins a validated future waypoint, with an explicit progress metric and queue-specific liveness proof before scale timing.
2. Prototype deterministic space-time reservations or cooperative corridor ownership around narrow gates and queue slots, making contention resolution explicit rather than repeatedly repairing independent routes.
3. Evaluate shared destination flow fields/corridor caches for high-fan-in services, while retaining per-person identity, wallets, speed, physical collision rules, saves and off-camera causality. Snapshot/publication optimization could be separately authorized afterward, but it must not be used to conceal unresolved movement liveness.

No option is selected by this report. R0.01 remains paused and S0.01 cannot be accepted from these measurements.

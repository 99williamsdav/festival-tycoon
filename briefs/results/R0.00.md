# R0.00 — fixture lifecycle kernel

Status: **Accepted.**

## Result

The headless R0.00 fixture now proves the approved outcome topology without adding gameplay hazards, economy, reward rules, art or UI controls:

- one deterministic active attempt contains exactly one fixture guest, staff member and performer;
- a forced multi-subject fixture death selects the first protected subject, freezes the edition, records one attributable casualty and failed attempt, and creates one open hearing;
- the single fixture-labelled Council Favour resolves that hearing once and creates one same-tier retry;
- a forced safe fixture outcome settles the retry once and advances exactly one fixture tier;
- every lifecycle field, counter, protected identity/role and completed outcome transaction ID participates in canonical hashing and persistence;
- legacy snapshots omit the optional lifecycle payload, retain their prior gameplay hash, and remain loadable.

`LifecycleTransitionCoordinator` stages each boundary on a restored candidate session, applies the command to that candidate, durably writes and validates the post-boundary autosave, and exposes the candidate only after save success. An injected failure leaves both the caller's session and the prior valid slot unchanged. Existing planning save semantics were not changed.

Tier IDs and the absence of a clock are explicitly labelled fixture-only. No roster size, weekend duration, real Favour source, carryover, reward, hazard, debrief, art or player-facing force control was introduced.

## Behaviour and implementation

- One owned portaloo starts at `(172,140)` and can be moved/rotated in preparation through its picked context panel. The placement command is atomic and free. It reserves the shell, door sweep, approach/exit and all ten potential physical queue slots/corridor, rejects obstacle/service/essential-route overlaps, and persists the approved site. Water, vendor, response-post and their growing queues respect this reservation.
- Each person has a deterministic combined need meter. A stable per-person visit choice is wee or poo. Guests approach and queue physically; one owner opens the door, enters, closes it during service, opens it for exit, and releases the next guest. Completion adds exactly 300 mL or 700 mL once, with separate counts, against a 12 L cap. Full tanks stop new admissions and release waiting visitors. An interrupted visitor releases ownership while the open door and an unavailable marker persist until they physically clear the interior; subsequent admission is blocked in that interval.
- Need, visit, queue, door, tank and interruption state enter authoritative saves/hashes. The immersion snapshot version is 3; new build/ruleset identity requires fresh current-version files and rejects unsupported older files without deleting them. A failed-weekend Council retry preserves the approved site/orientation but resets visits and tank.
- Smell begins above 25% full, decays within 8 m, and reduces nearby admitted guest satisfaction by at most 24 units per simulation second. Those constants, the 1,000-permille containment hook, need/service timing and waste volumes are fictional prototype balance values, not real-world measurements. The guest inspector names smell as a cause; the selected toilet shows textual occupancy/unavailability, wee/poo counts, litres and clamped percent full.
- The main GLB retains its open centreline/interior and 8 cm floor step. Runtime creates separate static wall/floor/roof/tank boxes and a moving door box under `DoorPivot` (0° closed, −110° open), never a whole-cabin hull. It switches exactly one green-circle/free or red-bar/occupied indicator at `OccupancySocket`. Existing person/pose assets are reused.

## Demonstration route

Launch the reviewed EXE with its adjacent data folder. In preparation, click the portaloo, select **Move**, rotate with comma/period or the on-screen Rotate control, click a valid grass site or Esc to cancel. Open the weekend. Guests whose need rises will physically queue, enter through the opening and close the door; the indicator turns red. After a visit, select the portaloo to read separate wee/poo counts and tank litres/percent. With enough accumulated waste, nearby guest inspectors show the bounded smell effect. Current-version preparation and occupied-state save/reload are also exercised by the scripted evidence above.

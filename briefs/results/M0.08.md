# M0.08 — Small physical queue completes purchases

Implementation status: **Implemented—verification pending**. Independent acceptance has not yet occurred.

## Delivered behaviour and invariants

Five simulation-owned attendees independently travel from the farm gate to five distinct physical queue
reservations beside the generic service point. Admission time and submission sequence come from the
authoritative session rather than a caller-supplied ordering key; stable agent ID resolves a genuine tie.
Late/rejoining attendees cannot displace an active customer. Index zero is the only service position; at most one active owner
exists, and it must also be the logical front member. Service cannot start until that attendee's
authoritative navigation position has arrived at slot zero.

The prototype sells the existing £3 basic drink at the small-bar authored duration: 90 festival seconds,
or 360 fixed 0.25-second ticks. Completion invokes the M0.04 atomic purchase path once, generating the
same balanced cash, revenue, stock and cost-of-goods entries. Completion rechecks the active owner's
exact service intention and physical presence. Queued/in-service routes and an active service-exit journey
are owned by attendee AI and cannot be externally retargeted. Exit ownership is released on physical
arrival, after which a new autonomous intention is accepted and remains persistable. A completed, failed
or abandoning attendee loses its queue reservation and is routed to its distinct deterministic exit;
queue compaction is deferred for one authoritative tick so that attendee takes an initial movement step.
Dense steering remains M0.09 scope.

Closure immediately cancels unfinished service without payment, empties logical membership, clears the
active owner/timer, releases every physical slot and routes affected guests to distinct exits. Reopening starts empty;
only a fresh attendee-AI intention can repopulate it. The commands used by the development scenario are
explicitly marked internal/fixture operations and are not exposed as player controls.

Queue membership/order, authoritative admission tick/sequence, per-agent intention/action, physical reservations, service owner/timer,
configuration, completion sequence, navigation destinations/routes, wallets, stock and transactions all
enter snapshots, persistence validation and canonical hashing. Missing queue fields in older v1 payloads
migrate to an empty queue collection while retaining the prior hash format. Restore validation rejects
closed queues with retained state, duplicate cells/reservations, incoherent member destinations/actions,
duplicate deterministic exit assignments across all queue-owned attendees (including future departures),
duplicate active exit ownership, and an active owner who is not physically present at the counter with
the exact service intention before reconstruction/hash comparison. If the logical front loses or changes
its service intent before service begins, it is deterministically abandoned and routed to its unique exit;
it is never charged, and the remaining eligible queue continues.

## Limitations

- This is one queue, one service position and one generic £3 purchase. It intentionally has no needs,
  preferences, staff, multiple tills, vendors, satisfaction or ATM behaviour.
- Sparse queue and exit destinations are distinct, and counter handoff is delayed one authoritative tick
  rather than measuring clearance. The default repaired scenario's closest observed pair was approximately
  354 mm apart. There is no general collision avoidance or dense-crowd pressure behaviour; that belongs to M0.09.
- Attendees have no rig or walking animation. Movement is the existing position-only prototype.
- Automated visual evidence was inspected, but subjective hands-on observation of the full 74-second
  accelerated close/reopen demonstration remains for independent review. Nothing was pushed and M0.09
  was not started.

## Independent review repairs

Independent Astra-low review rejected initial commit `d37ae50` with five reproducible lifecycle defects.
This repair replaces caller-trusted admission ordering, preserves the current owner at the front, routes
failed/abandoning customers clear before compaction, protects and revalidates the service intention,
validates queue/navigation cross-fields before restore, and continues exported verification from the
five-payment moment through all five completed departures. Each supplied reproduction now has an
adversarial regression. Independent re-review of the repair commit remains pending.

A second Astra-low pass found three persistence/intent gaps in repair commit `996caf9`. This follow-up
adds explicit lifecycle ownership for service-exit journeys, rejects retargeting only while that ownership
is active, releases it on arrival so later autonomous intents can be saved and restored, enforces unique
active exit indices in runtime and restore validation, and requires the exact service intent at both
service start and the final completion boundary. The supplied post-abandon retarget/save, duplicate-exit
rehash and wrong-intent completion reproductions are now adversarial regressions. The counter-handoff
description above also states the implemented one-tick behavior without claiming measured clearance.
Independent re-review of this follow-up remains pending.

A final repair closes two additional adversarial gaps: restore now rejects duplicate fixed exit indices
across every queue-owned attendee even when neither attendee owns an active departure reservation, and a
front attendee whose intent is wrong before service starts is abandoned/released without payment so later
eligible attendees can complete. Exact regressions mutate attendee 5 to exit index 0 before departure and
mutate attendee 4 to `ai.other-intent` before arrival. Full Debug, headless, exported-runtime, persistence,
failure, retarget, malformed-snapshot, and prior-milestone checks pass. Independent review remains pending.

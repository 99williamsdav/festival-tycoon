# M0.07 result — Single-agent navigation

Status: Implemented and self-verified on 14 September 2026; independent acceptance review pending.

## Implemented boundary

The simulation owns one generic attendee on a static 256 by 256 traversal grid covering the canonical
128 by 128 metre site in 0.5 metre cells. Authoritative positions are integer millimetres. Sparse,
sorted terrain overrides record the baseline surface, walkability, cost, elevation and slope metadata;
rainfall, wear and crowd effects are not implemented.

`DeterministicPathfinder` uses eight-neighbour A* with orthogonal cost 1000, diagonal cost 1414 and
destination-cell terrain cost in permille. A diagonal is rejected unless both adjacent orthogonal cells
are walkable. The admissible octile heuristic uses the grid's minimum walkable terrain cost, rounds the
minimum orthogonal and diagonal edge costs individually exactly as actual edges do, and then multiplies
those edge costs by their respective step counts. This remains a lower bound even at very small positive
costs. An improved closed node is reopened, so positive costs below the default remain optimal. The stable
open-set tie-break is lowest total F cost, then lowest scaled octile H estimate, then
lowest Z, then lowest X; neighbour discovery order is north, east, south, west, north-east, south-east,
south-west, north-west. Searches stop after at most all 65,536 cells.

The attendee walks at 120 integer millimetres per festival second: 30,000 micrometres per 0.25-second
authoritative tick. Segment origin, segment progress and a fractional terrain-cost remainder prevent
retarget snaps and systematic rounding bias. A new AI intent routes from the occupied cell but begins its
first segment at the exact current integer position, including after save/resume. State is Idle,
Travelling, Arrived or NoRoute. The only destination input is the internal
`SetAgentDestinationCommand` emitted by the scripted attendee-AI fixture. No player destination,
click-to-move or direct attendee control was added.

The Godot presenter reads integer simulation snapshots and visually interpolates between them. The
approved unrigged generic attendee GLB has no animation or root motion and cannot modify authoritative
position. Runtime wall time only determines how many fixed ticks to request at the 1x-equivalent rate of
80 ticks per real second.

Traversal metadata plus each agent's destination, full route, route index, integer segment origin,
segment progress, remainder,
action and last bounded-search count are included in save snapshots and canonical hashes. Sessions that
predate navigation retain their previous save JSON/checksum and hash contract by omitting the optional
navigation section.

## Limitations

- Physical mouse/keyboard interaction and subjective walking feel were not exercised. Pace is verified
  exactly by authoritative ticks and the exported presenter runs at the specified 80 ticks/real second.
- The attendee has no rig or locomotion animation; position interpolation is the only visual movement.
- No social AI, construction, crowd pressure/separation, route cache, navmesh, rainfall/wear accumulation
  or full stuck rescue was added. M0.08 was not started, and nothing was pushed.

## Independent review repairs

Independent review of commit `1106b15` correctly rejected acceptance on three points. This repair:

- persists and hashes an integer segment origin, so same-target and changed-target AI retargets continue
  from the exact current authoritative position instead of snapping toward the occupied cell centre;
- relocates the inherited canonical service point from `(4,18)` to `(29,-5)` metres, south-east of the large
  barn, and derives the navigation destination from that canonical farm object. A structural test proves
  that the unobstructed shortest route intersects the barn while the authoritative route stays out of the
  footprint and uses the west-side clearance; and
- scales A*'s heuristic by the minimum walkable cost and reopens improved closed nodes. A low-cost corridor
  regression proves a detour that initially moves away from the target beats the direct grass route.

The file-backed retarget save/load test verifies the new segment origin survives serialization and yields
the same continuation hash. The pre-existing M0.05 save demonstration also passed with identical loaded
and resumed hashes. Final independent repair acceptance remains pending.

A second independent review found that the first low-cost heuristic repair still rounded only after
summing all estimated steps, while real edges round individually. At cost 1 this could overestimate nine
diagonal edges as 12 instead of their actual total of 9 and allow early non-optimal target termination.
The heuristic now rounds one minimum orthogonal edge and one minimum diagonal edge before multiplying by
the step counts. The exact seeded 10 by 10 reproduction now returns the Dijkstra-established optimum cost
11 instead of 12. A fresh exported post-fix run also passed rendered/headless equivalence at tick 1831
with matching hash `9b4060d9d981d99060955e2b25b08a7c3a32f6f19c49841e794cbe50c4de3d9f`;
all three regenerated images were visually inspected, and the already-committed equivalent evidence set
was retained to keep this algorithm-only repair scoped to fix, test and documentation. Final independent
acceptance remains pending.

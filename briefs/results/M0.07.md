# M0.07 result — Single-agent navigation

Status: Implemented and self-verified on 14 September 2026; independent acceptance review pending.

## Implemented boundary

The simulation owns one generic attendee on a static 256 by 256 traversal grid covering the canonical
128 by 128 metre site in 0.5 metre cells. Authoritative positions are integer millimetres. Sparse,
sorted terrain overrides record the baseline surface, walkability, cost, elevation and slope metadata;
rainfall, wear and crowd effects are not implemented.

`DeterministicPathfinder` uses eight-neighbour A* with orthogonal cost 1000, diagonal cost 1414 and
destination-cell terrain cost in permille. A diagonal is rejected unless both adjacent orthogonal cells
are walkable. The stable open-set tie-break is lowest total F cost, then lowest octile H estimate, then
lowest Z, then lowest X; neighbour discovery order is north, east, south, west, north-east, south-east,
south-west, north-west. Searches stop after at most all 65,536 cells.

The attendee walks at 120 integer millimetres per festival second: 30,000 micrometres per 0.25-second
authoritative tick. Segment progress and a fractional terrain-cost remainder prevent systematic rounding
bias. State is Idle, Travelling, Arrived or NoRoute. The only destination input is the internal
`SetAgentDestinationCommand` emitted by the scripted attendee-AI fixture. No player destination,
click-to-move or direct attendee control was added.

The Godot presenter reads integer simulation snapshots and visually interpolates between them. The
approved unrigged generic attendee GLB has no animation or root motion and cannot modify authoritative
position. Runtime wall time only determines how many fixed ticks to request at the 1x-equivalent rate of
80 ticks per real second.

Traversal metadata plus each agent's destination, full route, route index, segment progress, remainder,
action and last bounded-search count are included in save snapshots and canonical hashes. Sessions that
predate navigation retain their previous save JSON/checksum and hash contract by omitting the optional
navigation section.

## Limitations

- Physical mouse/keyboard interaction and subjective walking feel were not exercised. Pace is verified
  exactly by authoritative ticks and the exported presenter runs at the specified 80 ticks/real second.
- The attendee has no rig or locomotion animation; position interpolation is the only visual movement.
- No social AI, construction, crowd pressure/separation, route cache, navmesh, rainfall/wear accumulation
  or full stuck rescue was added. M0.08 was not started, and nothing was pushed.

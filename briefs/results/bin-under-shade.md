# Bins beneath the shade tent — 12 October 2026

Bins can now share the marquee's open canopy ground. The overlap check compares both placements symmetrically, so placing/moving a bin under a tent and placing/moving a tent over a bin use the same rule. The bin's existing berth must stay clear of the tent's poles and guy-rope pegs. All other service overlaps, site/terrain/reserve restrictions and required access routes remain blocked as before. Nothing is automatically moved, and prices/refunds/stock are unchanged.

The tent retains its full reserved footprint for other furniture and its existing physical pole/peg cells. Bin solids and all four bin interaction-side routes remain in build/navigation validation. Shade coverage is unchanged. Where a bin occupies a resting position, the layout selects spare checkerboard cells within the existing shade in the original deterministic order, retaining 15 resting places; heat rest and rain shelter use the same derived positions. Existing layouts without a canopy bin keep their positions and behavior. No new art, saved fields or save-version change.

Verification: seven focused placement cases passed, then all 24 selected placement/marquee/build-layout regressions passed. This includes both object orders at all four rotations, bin/tent movement, all pole/peg rejections, unchanged non-bin and bin/bin overlap rejection, reachable bin sides, 15 walkable shaded rest positions and preparing/running save restoration. The full-capacity test was also rerun with a bin occupying an original resting position: fifteen guests still take distinct tent places, the next rests by first aid, and the state restores. Logs: [regressions](../../reports/evidence/bin-under-shade/bin-shade-regression.log), [capacity](../../reports/evidence/bin-under-shade/bin-shade-capacity.log). Game build verified separately. No manual playthrough or new Windows export was commissioned for this change; validation is authoritative placement/navigation testing rather than a rendered mockup.

The existing bin berth and required route checks still apply: a bin cannot occupy a pole/peg clearance or block the tent's required central access point. Other canopy furniture has not been enabled. Unrelated dirty imports, outfit source and UID files are preserved.

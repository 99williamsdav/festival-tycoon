# M0.10 — Crowd benchmark and foundation gate

Implementation status: **Measured; gate has material performance failures**. The original 1,200 full-fidelity target is retained and marked Fail. The evidence supports 200 as the near-term reliable full-fidelity baseline and 500 as a functional stress/optimization ceiling, not a release capacity claim.

Implementation and measurement QA: continuing builder agent. Independent acceptance review is pending; this result does not mark M0.10 accepted.

## Benchmark contract

`CrowdBenchmarkFixture` runs eight staggered production `GameSession` queue simulations as independent service destinations. Every attendee uses the accepted physical-arrival lifecycle, authoritative 850–1150 permille walking-speed variation, normal pathfinding/separation, wallets, stock, service transactions and exits. Narrow and wide fixtures differ only in barrier opening width (3 versus 17 half-metre cells); demand, stock, service duration, starts, destinations and seeds are unchanged. Blocked guests are never despawned and assisted recovery is zero.

The standard headless window is 200 warm-up ticks followed by 300 measured ticks; completion then runs to a declared 20,000-controller-tick bound for scales below 1,200. The 1,200 fixture proved too expensive even to initialize and warm normally: an attempted 200-tick warm-up was stopped after roughly 25 minutes. Its recorded diagnostic therefore uses an explicitly non-comparable 4-tick warm-up and 12 early measured ticks, stops at tick 16, and reports 1,200 unfinished. It is evidence of the structural limit, never a percentile or completion pass.

## Practical recommendation and bounded follow-up

- Treat **200 full-fidelity attendees** as the reliable near-term gameplay baseline at 1× on this machine.
- Treat **500** as an M0 stress/optimization fixture only. It remains functionally correct but fails the tick-cost and 4× targets.
- Preserve **1,200 perceived/visible attendees** as an eventual ambition, not a currently supported full-fidelity capacity.
- A separate optimization brief should measure shared route/corridor caching, staggered planning budgets, destination flow fields, and simulation LOD/aggregation for distant/non-critical attendees. It must retain authoritative money, occupancy and visible-agent causality and re-run this unchanged matrix. No such rewrite was attempted in M0.10.

## Limitations

- The benchmark uses eight isolated production service sessions to supply multiple destinations; it does not yet model cross-destination decision switching in one world.
- Rendered evidence presents one destination's meshes and advances all destinations; off-camera agents use identical simulation logic but are not all drawn.
- The inspected 500 screenshot confirms the farm, HUD and benchmark disclosure render correctly, but attendees have not yet entered the visible farm. It must not be read as proof that 500 simultaneously visible meshes are legible or performant.
- Allocation rate, draw-call count and GPU timing are unmeasured at this gate. Frame time is process-level Godot presentation timing, not a GPU profiler capture.
- The 1,200 early-tick sample is too short and too early to characterize steady-state tick percentiles. Its initialization/warm-up behavior and incomplete status are the evidence.

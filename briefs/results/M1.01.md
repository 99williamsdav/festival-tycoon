# M1.01 — Campaign shell, eight weeks and finance

Implementation status: **Implemented — verification pending.** This work stops at Opening Check and does not start M1.02.

## Delivered behaviour

- A new campaign deterministically generates a fictional name and one of four code-native paper-tab palettes, remains immediately renamable, and uses the fixed inherited Lower Wittering Farm site. Campaign seed and derived site seed are authoritative.
- Festival name and palette persist in the save payload/checksum but are deliberately excluded from the gameplay hash. Site/seed, phase/week, finance, loan, commitments, ledger, digests and dismissed campaign-tip IDs are authoritative and hashed.
- Edition one begins at Planning W8 with £800 cash funded by £800 starter-loan principal. The five-edition loan forecasts £160 principal plus £64 interest at settlement. These £224 liabilities are not paid or accrued weekly.
- `Basic administration and cover` is the one authored baseline commitment at the existing £40 content default. Confirmation schedules payment for the next advance; a second normal confirmation rejects. The W8 advance pays it once through a balanced two-entry transaction, leaving £760 cash and £800 outstanding principal.
- Exactly eight manual `Advance Week` operations reach Opening Check. Editing/render time advances no authoritative tick. Digests state only transition, actual payments, remaining commitments, cash, debt and settlement warning; there are no invented acts, offers, tickets or guests.
- Every advance validates first, writes through the existing three-slot atomic autosave rotation, and mutates only after the save succeeds. Injected failure preserves the previous valid slot and leaves week/state unchanged.
- Existing M0 v1 saves remain supported: the M1 campaign payload is an optional v1 extension. Saves without it reconstruct and retain their legacy gameplay hash; the checked-in M0 fixture continued to pass.

Content identity remains the accepted catalogue revision/hash `d7e7597670c2f9bc2552fa5df29f4afe294270e346643160e92feb1436bb1dd9`; no balance values or graphic assets changed. Save compatibility build identity is `0.0.1-m1.01`; the original `ToolchainSmoke` string remains its intentionally fixed M0.01 boundary proof.

# R0.05y — shared queued-service choice

Implemented 29 September 2026 in the user-authorized bounded queue-choice slice. One deterministic policy now compares travel, physical FIFO wait, earlier approaching demand and own service time for interchangeable water taps and toilets. It uses current route position, actual remaining owner work, ordinal-ID ties, 160-tick staggered review and a strict 120-tick switching margin. Approachers hold no remote place; a switching queued person forfeits the old physical place. An entering/using/leaving owner remains in service. The toilet review tick is authoritative and persisted; full, interrupted, incompatible, physically capped and unreachable choices are handled separately. Food and bar are single-instance/product-specific, and the generic queue fixture, first aid, stage, entrance and response posts are not interchangeable-choice adapters.

Changed: `src/Festival.Simulation/QueuedServiceChoice.cs`, `MedicalIncident.cs`, `WaterQueueGrowth.cs`, `Toilets.cs`, `Immersion.cs`, the labelled simulation fixture, `game/Main.cs` and its capture partial, focused tests, this brief/index/progress record and four screenshots under `reports/evidence/R0.05y/`. The fixture starts a Build campaign with two toilets and two taps, forms long physical lines, switches one queued seeker of each type, checks old-place forfeiture/owner retention and freezes the state for a labelled native capture. This is diagnostic evidence, not ordinary autonomous-play footage.

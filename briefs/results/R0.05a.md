# R0.05a result — water foundations

Status: **Player-placement correction implemented and independently re-reviewed; human acceptance pending.** This result covers water foundations only, not staff slots, band timetable, perk draft, or R0.06.

## Decisions and implementation

- Original `water.main` provides the guaranteed baseline and can be moved during preparation. Preparation can place up to two extra taps at player-chosen grass cells. Each has a saved identity, independently owned 10-place physical-arrival FIFO, visible 10-place overflow, one drinker, route, asset and pick inspector. Guest selection uses estimated personal walk ticks plus effective remaining queue/service ticks, with stable ID tie-breaking and bounded pre-queue reassessment. No tap-count pressure divisor, plumbing or flow network was added.
- Ordinary personal rates remain 8/12/16/20 thirst/tick. Council sharing caps this personal baseline at 12; the tower then adds 4. Tower alone: 12/16/20/24. Sharing plus tower: 12/16/16/16. Heat relief is a quarter of final thirst relief. The tower therefore helps even without sharing, and sharing still slows faster drinkers. These are prototype tunables.
- The exact dual-approved [rustic tower v1](../../art/reviews/R0.05a/ASSET_APPROVALS.md) is integrated 9.7 m east of the farmhouse centre with a 3.5 × 3.5 m navigation reservation and pickable inspector. Its game GLB SHA-256 matches the approved runtime source: `986FF8737828C8310F065EB7A22CCABBF6003E7431DDC87DB5561D32BFA04151`. Extra points reuse the approved standpipe v4. Effects are free preparation choices in this foundation only; no random draft, cost or entitlement has been authorized or implied.
- Preparation/medical state and canonical hash include the layout, tower ownership, queue/overflow membership, per-person chosen point, owner and drink timer. Extra stands and tower persist through same-tier retry; attempt-scoped Council sharing does not. Load clears stale selected standpipe/tower objects when the loaded layout lacks them.

## Player-placement correction — 25 September 2026

- The fixed west/east preparation buttons were replaced by `ADD TAP` and `MOVE ORIGINAL TAP`. The player points and clicks on a snapped grass cell, with green/red footprint and full queue/overflow markers, reason text, right-click/Esc cancellation and autosaved placement. New extra IDs are `water.extra-1/2`; legacy west/east IDs remain load-compatible but are not new placement choices. Validation rejects non-grass/out-of-bounds cells, overlaps with stands/queues/stage/equipment/medical facilities/tower, and placements that sever routes to water or first aid. The original tap's moved cell is used by its visual, pick, navigation obstacle, service queues, save and retry.
- Selection estimates shortest traversable route time using the person's walking speed and terrain costs, adds each physical queue member's remaining thirst at their effective Council/tower rate, then the seeker's own service time. Pre-queue seekers may switch only after a staggered 160-tick review and a 120-tick improvement; physical queue and overflow members retain arrival-order ownership. Deterministic tie-breaking remains by tap ID.
- Fresh review found two bounded defects: a same-ID extra tap kept its old visual/pick transform when loading a different layout, and the legacy fixed-site creation command could bypass the new two-extra capacity limit. World sync now moves existing visual/pick objects; legacy west/east *creation* is rejected while existing saves remain load-compatible. A saved-layout-B rendered regression checks the old pick is absent and the new pick/inspector works. New tests reject mixed legacy/new commands without poisoning restore, explicitly restore old west/east saves, and preserve moved-original plus arbitrary extra cells through same-tier retry.

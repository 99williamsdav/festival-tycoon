# R0.05 — builder checkpoint (not acceptance)

The real death/hearing/Favour retry backend and one-weekend community-water trade-off are implemented. A real incident starts with one Council Favour; spending it after a fatal hearing creates one same-tier attempt. The retry resets cash to £800 and consumable stock to 40, discards unused purchased stock, preserves the uniquely owned rig and known contacts, and expires rentals/work contracts. The failed casualty and hearing remain in history. No loan, safe early closure, success reward, tier advance, score or endless mode was added.

The optional community commitment is once per campaign, made before a Hot weekend. The preparation UI discloses the ordinary 8/12/16/20 rates, the cap of 12 thirst units/tick, longer queues and the full-weekend Favour condition. The live tap displays the effective capped pace. The claim is recorded with stable `community-water-favour:{campaignId}` identity only on completion of the committed weekend; failure yields no claim and a retry cannot recommit. Both the preparation and lifecycle state are included in save/restore and authoritative hashing.

Prepared-campaign saves now use the `r0.05-hearing-v1` directory and compatibility namespace. Older R0.04 development saves are left untouched but are incompatible with the new authoritative preparation/lifecycle schema. A rehired maintenance worker retains the same person and wallet identity across retries; repeated hiring cannot create extra wallets.

An explicitly headless `CreateR005RetryEconomyFixture` starts with two test-only Favours so two consecutive fatal retry reset cycles can be tested. Normal campaigns still start with exactly one. The second natural fatal retry cannot yet be reached through normal gameplay: the only extra Favour requires finishing a full weekend, and R0.06 progression is excluded here. This fixture tests the economics without moving the approved earning boundary.

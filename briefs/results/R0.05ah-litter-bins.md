# R0.05ah — litter, bins and steward cleanup

Implemented 1 October 2026 under [the producer-authorized brief](../R0.05ah-litter-bins.md). Approved slatted-bin and litter/wasp handoffs are copied with [source provenance](../../assets/source/environment/LITTER-PROVENANCE.md). No replacement graphics or sting damage were introduced.

Optional draft bins cost £15 at atomic Start, hold 20 normal pieces and accept overflow. Every actual completed consumption creates one stable purchase-backed waste identity; partial consumption creates none. Hidden seeded guest personality, reachable approach/detour and urgent needs govern the disposal decision. Packaging is carried briefly, physically binned after arrival/action or dropped at the current position. All physical departure/ejection paths release carried waste before marking departure, including the final exit that freezes the edition.

Stewards sweep near their current post after four nearby pieces or an eligible bin. Automatic sweeps are limited to 6 targets/30 seconds within 8 m; manual Clean up to 12 targets/60 seconds within 16 m. Both return to post. Safety recall releases exclusive target ownership without deleting waste. Emptying requires at least 18/20 pieces (90%), including manual requests; physical emptying takes 3 seconds. No bags/collection simulation or maintenance-worker cleanup. Ground nuisance and wasp annoyance use the bounded tunables in the brief; urgent needs and medical/argument/fight routes keep priority.

Every ground piece and overflow record has an unscaled instance in one of three shared-material MultiMesh batches. No visible cap, per-piece physics or per-frame authoritative scans. Derived person/waste/bin caches and nearby spatial buckets support claims/effects; cleanup tests a bounded candidate set. Approved part/full overlays are mutually exclusive at identity. Five tiny unscaled wasps per full bin use simulation time and vanish when emptied. At ordinary game zoom they are subtle and at wide field zoom are not reliably readable; the inspector is authoritative.

Current-version waste, carried actions and cleanup claims participate in saves and canonical hashes. Restore rejects malformed identity/completion, coordinates, routes and ownership. Retry preserves bin layout and clears waste/cleanup. The ordinary save namespace is v11; older files are untouched. A legacy nullable DTO omission preserves the checked-in nonpersonal v1 checksum fixture without claiming old Build-save migration.

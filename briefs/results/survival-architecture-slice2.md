# Survival architecture — policies and campaign state

The second slice adds one implemented Survival profile with narrow progression and fatal-incident consequence policies, plus seven existing danger timings selected through that profile. The fixed two-tier unlock rule, first-death freeze, casualty/hearing transaction order, no-Favour resolution and retry consequences are preserved. Hazard detection, navigation, event construction and random draws remain in their existing domains. No future Classic profile, rarity/balance, menu, ownership or economy is implemented.

`PersistentCampaignSnapshot` is a cloned, derived view of existing campaign identity/site/name/colours, actual cash/debt/Favour, water-share use, standing, knowledge, earlier relationships/popularity/gigs and purchased sound rig. It creates no second authoritative store or new persistence fields. Per-festival Preparation keeps bookings, hires, stock, rentals, layout and current performances. Transition settles current gigs in the existing order; stored earlier gigs are documented as festival-opening history. Mutation of the returned arrays does not alter authority.

The profile resolves current festival definitions and progression, and supplies typed fatal-consequence and incident-timing seams. Future Classic can provide another defined profile, progression and consequence policy; organic growth, nonterminal reputation consequences and incident rarity still require actual implementation, validation and balance work. These are extension points, not a playable mode or a claim of fully generic campaign validation. Existing `CampaignMode.Classic` and `HandsOn` remain compatibility adapters for control roles; only Survival is an implemented ruleset.

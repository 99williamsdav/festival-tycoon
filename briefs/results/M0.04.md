# M0.04 result — Atomic cash purchase and stock accounting

Status: Accepted on 8 September 2026. Implemented and self-verified by Codex on Windows x64, then independently reviewed by Astra Low with no actionable defects.

## Reusable purchase contract

`PurchaseItemCommand` travels through the existing `GameSession.Execute(CommandEnvelope)` path. The envelope target is the festival-owned service stock entity; the payload contains a stable transaction ID, buyer wallet owner, festival cash owner, integer-penny unit price and integer quantity.

Validation completes before mutation, in this order after the shared command/campaign/phase/tick/submission checks:

1. nonzero unused transaction identity and positive price/quantity;
2. buyer wallet, festival cash owner and service stock identity;
3. service ownership by the supplied festival owner;
4. checked sale and inventory-cost multiplication;
5. sufficient buyer cash and stock quantity; and
6. checked festival cash capacity.

A rejection changes no wallet, festival cash, stock, ledger, command history, identity set, entity allocation or submission sequence. An acceptance constructs and verifies the complete immutable transaction, then commits cash, stock, transaction identity and ledger together. Future service completion code in M0.08 should submit this same command/operation rather than duplicate its finance logic.

The three create commands in `AtomicPurchaseFixture` are explicitly development-only setup primitives for wallets, festival finance and owned stock. They do not model ticket sales, procurement or the annual economy.

## Accounts and sign convention

Ledger amounts are integer pennies. Debit is positive and credit is negative:

- assets and expenses increase with positive/debit entries;
- revenue increases with negative/credit entries; and
- an asset reduction is negative/credit.

For a 300p sale of an item with 120p cost basis, the immutable transaction contains six explicit owner/account entries:

| Owner | Account | Amount | Meaning |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Guest | CashAsset | -300p | guest cash decreases |
| Guest | GuestSpending | +300p | guest expense increases |
| Festival | CashAsset | +300p | festival cash increases |
| Festival | SalesRevenue | -300p | sales revenue is credited |
| Festival | CostOfGoodsSold | +120p | stock cost becomes expense |
| Festival | InventoryAsset | -120p | inventory value decreases |

All entries sum to zero. Sales revenue remains 300p even though cost of goods sold is 120p; neither is conflated with the cash transfer or remaining stock value.

## Authoritative state and hashing

The canonical session hash schema advances from 1 to 2 and includes, in deterministic order:

- wallets sorted by owner entity ID with cash balances;
- festival finance owners sorted by entity ID with cash balances;
- owned service stocks sorted by service entity ID with owner, quantity and unit cost basis;
- completed transactions in deterministic accepted submission order, including stable transaction/command identities, tick, participants, service, quantity, price and every ordered ledger entry; and
- the existing entity allocator, command identity/history, fixture records and non-cosmetic PRNG streams.

The M0.02 demonstration hashes consequently change because the authoritative hash schema changed. Its same-build equivalence, repeatability, expiry behavior and batching invariance remain intact.

## Acceptance mapping

1. `PurchaseTransfersCashConsumesStockAndBooksBalancedEntries` proves 500p becomes 200p, festival cash becomes 300p and stock drops by one.
2. The same test asserts separate -300p SalesRevenue, +120p CostOfGoodsSold and -120p InventoryAsset entries and a zero transaction sum.
3. `ExactCashSucceedsWhileInsufficientCashAndEmptyStockAreAtomicRejections` proves exact cash reaches zero and both failure hashes, submission sequences and transaction counts remain unchanged.
4. `AcceptedCommandAndTransactionIdentitiesAreBothIdempotent` retries the accepted command, then retries its transaction ID under a new command; neither changes state or adds a transaction.
5. `LastItemGoesToFirstBuyerInSubmissionOrder` proves the first submitted buyer receives the only item and the second remains uncharged with `OutOfStock`.
6. `InvalidAmountsAndMissingIdentitiesRejectWithoutMutation` covers negative price, negative quantity, missing buyer, missing festival owner and missing service. `CumulativeTransfersReconcileAcrossMultiplePurchases` proves three sales transfer exactly 800p from combined wallets to festival cash, consume exactly three units, credit 800p revenue and leave every transaction balanced.

`TransactionIdentityAndLedgerAreAuthoritativeHashInputs` additionally proves economically identical sessions using different transaction IDs have different authoritative hashes.

## Limitations and stop boundary

- This brief models one festival-owned service sale only. Vendor books/revenue share, cards, ATMs, loans, taxes, refunds and procurement remain deferred.
- Unit cost basis is a fixed integer-penny amount per unit; cost-layer selection and restocking policy await inventory work.
- Opening balances are fixture state, not ledger transactions, so reconciliation uses known opening snapshots plus completed transfers.
- Sessions execute commands serially on the deterministic simulation boundary; no cross-thread mutation contract is claimed.
- No queue or service timer invokes purchases yet; M0.08 must reuse this operation after its service completes.
- Manual QA is command-line only because M0.04 has no visual behavior.
- M0.05 was not started.

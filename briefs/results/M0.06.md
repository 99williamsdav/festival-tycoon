# M0.06 result — Farm scene, camera and persistent overlay

Status: Implemented—verification pending on 14 September 2026. Implemented and self-verified by Codex on Windows x64; independent review remains required before Accepted.

## Implemented boundary

`LowerWitteringFarmScenario` supplies an immutable 128 x 128 metre scenario/read model with six stable IDs, authored positions, quarter-turn orientations, inherited/open state, permanence, movement and selection flags. The fixed compact blockout occupies the central 64 x 64 metre field. The farmhouse, small barn and large barn are permanent and immovable; the trailer stage and generic service point are inherited fixed fixtures; the gate is visibly open. This brief adds no procedural gameplay layout, construction, purchasing, refurbishment, paths, wear, mud, navigation, guests or management simulation.

The Godot presenter instantiates approved project-owned GLBs and registers each pickable `StaticBody3D` instance ID against its read-model object. Ray picking resolves that registry only; mesh and node names never provide gameplay identity. Presentation selection, camera focus/orientation/zoom and screenshots remain outside simulation state. A paused `GameSession` hash is displayed and checked throughout the runtime demonstration.

The orthographic camera has four fixed 90-degree yaw orientations at a high angled view, world-relative keyboard/middle-drag pan clamped to 24 metres, and wheel/button zoom clamped from 38 to 82. Q/E and visible buttons rotate; WASD/arrows and middle drag pan; wheel and +/- buttons zoom; Space/the PAUSED button report the current foundation pause state. Selection persists across camera changes. Left click selects registered objects and an empty-world ray clears selection.

The persistent code-native HUD uses cream paper panels, aligned modern text, top time/pause/view controls, a right selection inspector, a bottom control legend, and a procedural amber selection disc. It was rendered at 1280x720 and 1920x1080 with no clipped essential text.

## Approved art and provenance

## Limitations and manual-QA disclosure

- Codex manually inspected the generated Windows screenshots. Native-app UI automation was unavailable: the computer-use provider exposed no Windows applications and its native `getApp` API was disabled. Therefore physical mouse/keyboard pointer feel was not manually exercised by Codex. The exported runtime instead exercised real camera projection, physics ray queries, registry resolution, empty clearing, camera bounds and hash invariance at both resolutions. User/manual pointer-feel QA remains open and is why this row is not marked Accepted.
- The compact scene uses a flat tiled field, straight inherited track and perimeter hedges. Gentle slopes, wet ground, trees, terrain state, navigation and authored collision are future work.
- Buildings have presentation selection boxes only. Roof fade/cutaway, interior access and entrance overlays are excluded from this blockout.
- Lighting is fixed warm daylight. Art is approved low-poly blockout material, not claimed final production fidelity.
- The PAUSED control is intentionally a visible foundation-state confirmation; M0.06 does not invent a running simulation clock or speed controls.
- No M0.07 work was started, and nothing was pushed.

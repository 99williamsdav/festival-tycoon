# R0.01 — preparation and persistence implementation checkpoint

Status: **Accepted as a bounded R0.01 implementation after independent repair/re-review and a completed representative run.** Human-paced ten-minute playtesting and stable-60-FPS performance remain unverified/failed, respectively; this is not a crowd-capacity acceptance. P3 remains parked and unaccepted. This work uses existing production navigation and does not integrate TrafficProof.

## Delivered bounded slice

The default game route now opens preparation directly, retaining the campaign/farm identity and £800 opening loan/cash. Legacy eight-week planning saves and their capture route remain supported. Tier 1 owns 20 mandatory guests, Casey Vale (staff) and Alex Reed (performer); Tier 2 owns 40 guests and those two protected roles: **22/42 total**, with one stable identity and wallet per person. No command can remove guests or add fixture people on this route.

Start autosaves a validated candidate before replacing the visible session. All roster members then use the existing navigation system to walk from allocated gate-approach positions to field positions, and physically return during departure. No role is teleported, silently removed or exempted from movement. This intentionally modest arrangement has six explicit approach lanes; it does not demonstrate arbitrary congested layouts or production service queues.

The provisional schedule is 38,400 live ticks, divided equally into Friday/Saturday/Sunday: 160 festival minutes, eight real minutes at the unchanged 80 ticks/real second. Physical egress adds a short period. The approximately ten-minute full-attempt target includes ordinary preparation/pauses and remains **unverified by a full playtest**. Finished weekends expire contracts but do not yet advance tiers or create success rewards; those are later briefs.

## Offers and actual consequences

Seven seed-stable offers are derived without consuming random streams; the tier seed and accepted IDs persist. Each category accepts at most one offer per attempt. An owned rig prevents buying/renting a duplicate.

| Choice | Immediate cost | Demonstrated consequence |
|---|---:|---|
| Alex's folk or punk programme | £60/£65/£70, seed-derived, equal between genres | Fit changes individual guest satisfaction by 3,000 fixed-scale points and the bounded music-risk input |
| Casey's basic or extended sound shift | £20 / £40 | +400 / +800 music quality; paid work contract expires, contact remains |
| Buy / rent sound rig | £120 / £30 | +1,000 / +500 music quality; owned rig survives outcomes, rental expires |
| Stock delivery contract | £30 | Adds 50 nonperishable refreshment packs; one pack consumed per guest on physical arrival, unused packs persist |

The inherited opening stock is 40 packs at 60p unit basis. A consumed pack adds 200 satisfaction; it is an included arrival pack, not a simulated queue sale. No ticket revenue, post-failure income or reward is invented. Minimum act+worker commitment costs £80–£90, or £110–£120 including optional rental; all initial seed samples can afford this from £800. Repeated paid retries can exhaust money; no retry floor or free contract has been added.

Music satisfaction before the pack is `clamp(5000 + act quality + staff quality + rig quality + fit - 500*(tier-1), 0, 10000)`. Fit is +1,000 for matching genre and -2,000 otherwise; the bounded music-risk input is `clamp(5000 - pre-pack satisfaction, 0, 3000)`. No hazard consumes this risk yet and it is not a victory checklist. Needs/spatial listening/audio remain deferred.

No lethal chain is enabled, so essential-counter eligibility is currently vacuous. This is **not** evidence that R0.02–R0.04 counters exist or are affordable; each chain must establish that before eligibility. No Favour economy, lethal controls, safe closure, reward floor or P4 integration was added.

## Persistence and finance

The optional versioned preparation extension is hashed and stored through the existing save adapter. It includes tier/offer seed, attempt, named protected roster and progress, paid offers, inventory consumption, owned equipment, rentals, contacts and edition work contracts. Validation reconciles paid entitlements, cash, stock and fixed identities, and rejects malformed collection members or missing physical people. Existing campaign identity, inherited permanent farm objects and loan persist.

Payments immediately debit equipment/inventory/expense and credit cash through a balanced **transaction-change** ledger read model. Arrival pack consumption debits cost of goods and credits inventory. The separate `GetPreparationInventoryBalance()` explicitly supplies the inherited opening 40 units / £24 value plus purchased, consumed and remaining units; the transaction-change ledger alone is not an opening trial balance. Failure fixtures freeze ticks and preserve actual current cash/stock/owned property while expiring rental/work contracts. Fixture retry retains seed, revealed choices, roster identities and known contact, and requires newly paid act/work contracts. These clearly labelled headless hooks are persistence probes, not playable death/Favour logic, and are disabled on the normal route.

## Single targeted review repair

The fresh review identified two bounded findings. Departure/finished states previously became visible before a mandatory phase-boundary autosave, and inherited stock lacked an explicit opening inventory balance in the transaction ledger presentation.

`PreparationAdvanceCoordinator.AdvanceOne` now runs ordinary ticks directly but stages exactly the departure and finished boundary ticks in isolated candidates. It writes and validates the candidate through rotating autosave before returning the replacement session. A failed write retains the original tick, phase, contracts and physical state. The UI pauses progression, surfaces the failure and offers **RETRY SAVE**. Periodic autosave failures also surface. Finished settlement now occurs one tick after the last physical departure, making the upcoming boundary explicit without cloning every movement tick.

Two injected-failure tests cover departure and finished separately: the prior visible hash and newest valid autosave remain unchanged on failure, successful retry restores the exact committed boundary from the newest autosave, and rental/work contracts expire only at finished settlement. The inventory test proves opening + purchases − actual consumption = remaining both in units and pennies after save/restore.

Repair verification: the same focused filter above passed **35/35 in 6.322 seconds**. Subsequent solution build, including the bounded runtime measurement harness, passed with **0 warnings/errors**. Changed repair files: `Preparation.cs`, new `PreparationAdvanceCoordinator.cs`, `PreparationTests.cs`, `Main.Preparation.cs`, a measurement argument in `Main.cs`, and this result document. No TrafficProof or later gameplay was changed.

The new optional `--measure-preparation 2` capture mode runs a scripted full 42-person attempt at the unchanged 1× clock with an 11-minute hard bound. It records independent wall duration, tick-derived attained speed, overall and movement-only frame percentiles, simulation/presentation work percentiles, peak memory, physical completion, and exact restoration of the newest final autosave. It deliberately reports scripted preparation separately from the unverified human preparation/pause time; it cannot certify the ten-minute human playtest target.

The coordinator's bounded real-time run completed successfully: **42/42 admitted, 42/42 departed, final autosave hash exact, 39,068 ticks**, with **533.224 seconds (8m53s) live wall time** and **0.915843× attained speed**. Overall frame p50/p95/p99 was **16.681/17.340/255.178 ms**; during movement it was **16.698/259.658/264.221 ms**. Simulation/presentation work p95/p99 was **0.681/1.953 ms**; peak working set was about **323 MB**. This shows significant movement-period frame stalls and misses a strict stable-60-FPS interpretation; it is not a generalized scale pass. The remaining roughly one minute to a ten-minute complete human attempt is plausible but untested because this run scripted decisions and no ordinary pauses.

## Behaviour and source

The newspaper is removed but the completed `GameSession` remains frozen and unsaved behind the splash; HUD refresh cannot recreate the newspaper there. `game/Main.Splash.cs` keeps ordinary unfinished splash entry unchanged. Only the actual Enter button in pending state creates a whole `GameSession.CreateBookingCampaign(seed)`, with a cryptographically chosen seed distinct from all campaigns created by this menu in this process. It rebinds presenter/world/HUD, resets feedback/cues/selections/visual people and autosave cadence, and returns to Programme after the initial perk draft. No individual authoritative funds, Favour, draft, roster or result fields are selectively reset; a native assertion compares the entire authoritative hash against a direct factory instance with the chosen seed.

The existing `user://saves/r0.05n-booking-v1` namespace and `0.0.1-r0.05n-booking-v1`/`r0-booking-v1` compatibility identity are unchanged. Return/Enter do not write, delete or rotate a save. Later explicit Save/Start retain the existing named-slot/rotating-autosave overwrite policy; this is **not** a per-campaign archive of all prior terminal runs. Old files remain on disk unless a later ordinary save uses an existing slot. An incompatible load leaves the fresh campaign unchanged; a compatible explicit completed-save load still opens its terminal newspaper.

`game/Main.ResultsCapture.cs` adds a dedicated `--capture-r005p-newgame` development route starting from the current N booking factory/HUD; the historical `--capture-r005m-results` route remains M and still passes. The P route presses actual named Return/Enter and Choose UI controls, verifies unfinished entry did not reset, held menu did not reopen the newspaper, fresh seed/identity/tick/status/draft/plan/result and full factory hash, repeated completed-menu cycle, terminal manual-save byte stability, incompatible adapter read purity, and compatible explicit completed-file reload. It asserts all three fresh draft cards/Choose actions fit inside the viewport and full-height panel after deferred layout, then Choose reveals Programme/cost/Start inside the viewport.

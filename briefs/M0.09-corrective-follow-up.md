# M0.09 corrective follow-up — camera and arrival-based queueing

Approved after the M0.09 hands-on review and completed before M0.10. This is a bounded correction to the accepted foundation, not a new milestone.

## Required behavior

- WASD and arrow-key panning use view-relative screen axes at every camera rotation.
- The foundation queue begins beside the generic service-point counter rather than leaving a roughly 14 m visual gap.
- Choosing or rejoining a service sends an attendee toward the physical admission area but does not reserve logical order or a queue slot. Admission occurs only on physical arrival. All admissions allocate from one persisted/hashed per-queue sequence; command submission order is not queue order. Order is admission tick, authoritative arrival sequence, then stable ID solely as the deterministic tie-break. The active service owner cannot be displaced.
- Foundation attendees use stable authoritative walking speeds from 85% through 115% of the prototype base rate. Speed affects travel only, is persisted and hashed, and does not change service duration.
- The existing exact swept-grid, separation, save/load, deterministic replay and M0.06–M0.09 regression guarantees remain in force.

The farmhouse-shadow observation is deferred visual polish. M0.10 remains not started.

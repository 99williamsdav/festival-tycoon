| 1 Oct | **Implemented stuck toilets and broken taps:** each toilet visit has a 7.4% chance of the door jamming as the occupant tries to leave, and each tap use a 3.8% chance of the tap breaking as it is turned on (about a 90% chance of at least one per facility in a typical Tier 1 day, about 2.2 on average, no cap). A stuck person shouts for help, increasingly desperately, and loses satisfaction faster the longer they wait (1 a second, plus 1 every 15 s, up to 8); a steward frees them in 6 s at the door. Nothing forces a collapse, but in hot weather any portaloo cubicle doubles heat gain for every user, so ordinary heat and thirst can get them there. Someone who collapses while stuck stays inside, silent, with the usual collapse clock running; the medic cannot reach them. When a steward opens the door, the body is moved just outside the cubicle for first aid. A broken tap serves nobody; its user grumbles and goes elsewhere. Maintenance mends it in 12 s; without a maintenance hire a steward bodges it in 20 s to half flow, which maintenance can later mend. Queue choices add the expected wait (help's travel plus the work, or a pessimistic 60 s before anyone is on the way); a bodged tap doubles service time. ## 30 September: bin progression direction

The user approved **basic open-top general-waste bins** whose full state attracts **wasps**, followed by unlockable better **lidded bins** that contain smell and do not attract wasps. Two new open-top concepts were commissioned for review, **not models or gameplay implementation**. The earlier hooded-bin A/B comparison is not approved for production. Capacity, filling/disposal/collection behavior, precise smell effects, wasp consequences, prices and unlock conditions remain undecided. This is distinct from the existing request for **flies near smelly toilets**. Source: producer relay of the user's 30 September decision; follow-up [B-020](BACKLOG.md) and [design index](../assets/source/DESIGN_INDEX.md).

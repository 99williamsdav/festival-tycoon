## Reputation and unlock tiers

Discussed as a possible later progression layer after the present demo ending. The anti-grind concern is unresolved: repetition should not become a mandatory route to basic survival counters or unlocks. No reputation thresholds, rewards, Tier 2 implementation or profit carryover are approved here. The older annual prestige/Know-how catalogue is historical input, not this design.

## Wider Council choices

Possible authored, trade-off-bearing Favour sources raised in discussion include sharing electricity, choosing a local band, lending a worker or a cow-related community choice. None is an active claimable choice. Current gameplay has the explicit water-sharing route; Favours must not become cash-purchased bribes or repeat-farmable. Each future choice needs its own causal cost, eligibility, once-only behavior and approval.

## Evening, night and a real-looking clock

The requested clock/lighting direction is tracked as [B-009](BACKLOG.md). Actual night lighting, festival-time-to-clock mapping and longer days are not approved runtime changes. Do not silently map the current eight festival minutes to midday–23:00 or claim a day/night cycle from the present sunny field.

## Overtime anger and expanded worker response

Additional staff fatigue/overtime grievance could become a later causal risk with visible contract terms, warnings and counterplay. Current staff roles, paid weekend contracts and return-to-post behavior do not contain such a system. No overtime rate, anger timer or new death chain is approved.

## Character rig and animation exploration

Procedural rigs, richer dance and instrument/holding animations have been discussed as art and technical exploration, not a commissioned rebuild. Preserve the existing approved male/female bodies, poses and performance kits until a concept, compatibility plan and runtime budget are reviewed. A dancing-based band reaction is likewise deferred until dancing is real.

FLOW / FINANCIAL CONTRACT
Approved forecast remains before preparation. Phase 1 BandBooking covers all acts across all active stages plus ticket price. All lineup edits are free drafting; show cost that will be committed on confirmation, not a charge already taken.
Ticket control supports minus/plus AND accessible direct numeric entry in implementation. Final bounds/step are Builder/coordinator decisions.
Estimated ticket SALES RANGE is prominent with known maximum capacity. It is not an exact prediction, probability, or guaranteed lower bound. Show concise model-supported cues, not fake causal claims. Popularity attracts buyers; talent affects satisfaction, not an invented ticket-demand multiplier.
Conservative projected site-prep funds = existing balance + lower estimate of ticket sales * price - total proposed band spend. This low-end estimate is not a promised minimum.
NO programme-spend cap. Do not clamp selection/fees to an invented allowance. For low/negative projected funds, replace the final estimate note with amber: 'Risk: little left for site prep. You can still confirm.' Allow wrapping to two lines by reclaiming space above the budget; keep confirmation enabled for financial risk alone. For negative amounts say 'Projected shortfall' and show the signed value; do not disguise it as zero. Exact warning threshold and any insolvency handling are not specified here. Existing eligibility/slot-completeness validation can still explain genuine invalid selections.
Confirmation resolves actual sales without a mandatory waiting simulation. Persist one result; navigation/reload/repeated button activation must not create another sale or another payment. Receipt is immediately dismissible.
Phase 2 SitePrep funds = prior balance + actual ticket revenue - confirmed band spend. Example: 800 + (124*10) -225 =1815. Label bands paid and exclude those costs from charges at Start. Keep existing Build/Programme/Staff/Supplies, budget and opening checklist. The concept receipt sits over that flow, not a new mandatory checklist or currency. Actual site-prep background can remain the current farm view; the flat green concept background is for graphical clarity.
After sales, hide editing of ticket price/sales estimate in Programme and replace with actual sold count and current funds. Draft an act change, then show its financial/attendee consequence before applying. Do not rerun ticket sales for this action.

POST-SALE CHANGE CONTRACT
Compact modal compares old/new act and slot. Show attendee impact in plain language derived from the actual comparison. Major headliner loss has more disappointment than a comparable replacement or minor change. The supplied headline example is an illustrative warning state, not an assertion that a 0.5 rating difference mechanically causes a fixed penalty. For comparable changes use proportionate copy, e.g. 'A comparable replacement; less disruption for attendees.' For minor changes use the actual lower-impact description. Do not add numeric happiness losses until the model supplies them.
Show exact quoted band refund, replacement fee, net change, resulting site-prep funds before final confirmation. Example refund60 minus fee90 = net cost30; 1815-30=1785. Refund60 is PURELY illustrative, not a policy, rate, deposit, penalty schedule or new contract mechanic. Builder/coordinator must define authoritative numeric/refund rules. If unavailable, do not pretend a quote is final.
Keep current booking is safe/default focus; Escape/back cancels preview without mutation; Replace headliner commits once. Focus trapped in modal; restore originating row after dismissal. Removal without replacement uses the same consequence pattern and reflects the empty slot/opening requirement.
Full festival cancellation is a quiet secondary action in post-sale Programme, labeled 'Cancel festival…', last resort. Separate final confirmation must state actual attendee refunds and reputation loss before committing, with 'Keep festival' as default. No invented refund percentages, new currency, loan or contract system. Final numbers pending Builder/coordinator.

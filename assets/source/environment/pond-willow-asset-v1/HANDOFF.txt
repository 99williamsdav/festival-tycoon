MODEL / APPROVED SILHOUETTE
Curved leaning trunk with arching limbs feeding 13 asymmetrical foliage groups,
143 tapered hanging strands, folded narrow clumps and irregular long fringes.
The v2 water-reaching length was preserved, rather than a raised round crown or
short skirt. Lowest foliage Y=0.055, 0.020 m beneath the existing waterline .075.
Maximum height 4.704 m. Tree bounds about 3.957 m along local X (overhang) by
4.133 m across. Lowest strands and overlapping curtains approach/trail into water;
upper foliage reaches roughly 2.7 m inward from the bank's inner edge.
Generated concept painting has finer painterly leaf marks/reflections; production
geometry uses faceted opaque groups. It does not include painted reflections,
reflection cameras, wind, cloth, bones, particles or individual leaf simulation.

TECHNICAL COST
WillowTrunkBranches: 978 triangles / 525 source mesh vertices.
WillowHangingFoliage: 26,884 triangles / 23,166 source mesh vertices.
Total 27,862 triangles, two surfaces, ONE matte palette material; GLB 2,514,300 bytes.
Flat faceted normals and palette UV; nearest texture sampling; roughness .94.
Opaque double-sided material (Godot cull_mode=2), no alpha sorting/cutout cost.
All foliage is one mesh, not 143 runtime nodes/draws. Trunk/branches are the other.
This is more geometry than existing very simple farm trees. Native performance
has NOT been profiled; two surfaces alone are not a performance guarantee.

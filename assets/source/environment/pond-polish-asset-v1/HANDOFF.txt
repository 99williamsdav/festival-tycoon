SAFE SWIMMING CONTRACT
verification.json contains a convex 32-point safe_duck_root_polygon_local_xz,
approximating ellipse radii (1.55,1.45) centred at pond local origin. Both initial
roots are inside it. Every straight segment between points in this polygon is
contained; curved paths must also keep their complete trajectory inside it.
The boundary was sampled at 101 points per edge: minimum shore distance 1.5037 m,
minimum lily-pad-edge distance 0.5982 m. Allow 0.42 m swept radius for each duck;
no second shore inset is needed for this conservative polygon. Keep two roots
at least 0.85 m apart and avoid crossing trajectories. Don't use the entire
visible water polygon as an unconstrained centre path. Lily locations/radii
and full water boundary are in manifest.json if Builder needs more freedom.
No duck or wake collision/navigation bodies are implied or requested.

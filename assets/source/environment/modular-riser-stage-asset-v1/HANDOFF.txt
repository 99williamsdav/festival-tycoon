6.022 x 4.022 m; small deck fasteners reach .903. Deck substrate is continuous,

Twelve stout flat-foot supports, steel crossmembers directly under substrate,
underdeck cross-bracing, warm timber edge inserts and visible corner plates.
Two short rear-corner rails only, about .65 m above deck; centre/open sides clear.
No roof, tall wall, overhead beam, canopy, lighting rig, wheels or hitch.
These dimensions describe the model only.

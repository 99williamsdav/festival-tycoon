Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one concept-art sheet for a future Festival Tycoon game vehicle, a practical Blender modelling reference.
Scene/backdrop: isolated on a pale warm-grey background with only simple soft ground shadows; no scenery.
Primary request: ONE trendy independent speciality COFFEE VAN for a British countryside music festival, shown three times as the SAME vehicle from different angles. A charming, pretty, low-poly 3D aesthetic with tactile, carefully simplified materials and controlled bevels, never plasticky, blocky or photorealistic.
Subject: a believable compact converted panel van, recognisable vehicle proportions, short bonnet and cab, softly chamfered tall cargo body, four ordinary visible road wheels with dark tyres and cream metal hubs. Warm cream upper body and roof, deep teal lower body, sage service-hatch interior, restrained warm brass accent line, warm wood serving counter and modest brushed metal trim. An original understated speciality-coffee identity using tasteful small cream COFFEE lettering and a simple coffee-bean roundel; no real vehicle badge or real brand.
Service conversion: one broad top-hinged service hatch in the cargo side, raised on two slim supports, its panel acting as the awning. The opening is wide and tall enough to see a clear central standing bay for one future server; no person now. A narrow practical wood counter at the opening, espresso machine, one grinder with bean hopper, a small stack of paper cups, a very small fixed menu. Equipment grouped at an end of the counter so the open central standing space remains clear. Simple warm interior wall, accessible lower cabinetry, believable cab-to-cargo relationship. No clutter.
Composition/framing: landscape design sheet. A dominant large service-side FRONT three-quarter view occupies roughly two thirds of the sheet, entire vehicle including tyres and raised hatch fully inside the frame. Along the remaining area, two smaller views with ample breathing room: a REAR/opposite-side three-quarter view showing closed double rear doors and the plain non-service side, and a straight FRONT view. Orthographic-ish cameras, minimal perspective distortion, same scale relationships and same wheelbase, roof shape, body seams, paint boundaries, hub design, mirrors, headlights, hatch position and trim in every view. The small front view must show the raised hatch projecting on the correct service side. Exactly three views of one design, no extra alternatives or exploded parts.
Lighting/mood: soft daylight studio rendering, approachable countryside craft aesthetic, matte painted metal with subtle surface variation, readable faceted forms and gentle ambient occlusion. Detail remains economical enough for a small tycoon game asset.
Text: only small COFFEE identity lettering and restrained small menu marks on the van; no sheet title, technical callouts, model name or captions.
Constraints: concept only; no screenshots, game UI or gameplay statistics; no people; no giant roof sign; no potted plants, bunting, fairy lights, outdoor furniture, loose props or decor obscuring the vehicle silhouette; no oversized tyres, toy exaggeration, cyberpunk or food-truck box-on-chassis shape. Prioritise cross-view geometric consistency and a buildable vehicle.

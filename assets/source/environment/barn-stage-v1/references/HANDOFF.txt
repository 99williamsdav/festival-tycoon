BARN STAGE — CONCEPT REVIEW — 13 OCTOBER 2026

Design only, commissioned by the established producer on behalf of the user. Two practical alternatives for the EXISTING LARGE barn. User clarification requires roof/wall translucency whenever someone is inside, matching the marquee. No Blender build, game integration, tier assignment, unlock change or new field is included. The source currently calls the user's Duck Stage the Pond Stage; this package uses Duck/Pond only when identifying the reused kit. The proposed venue name is Barn Stage.

RECOMMENDATION: B — END-ON HALL
Put the existing 6 × 4 m modular riser at the east end, facing west along the barn. Keep the current shell footprint and roof silhouette. The longer indoor viewing rectangle leaves a useful front cross-aisle between the main entrance and a new front-left exit. Guests enter behind the audience instead of walking straight toward the band. The occupied translucent view makes the east gable and roof see-through, so the stage remains legible even from that side. Main tradeoff: the band is less visible through the entrance when the building is opaque; the fade is essential, not optional. Stage access needs new barn-local routing even though the stage mesh and performance kit are reused. B also relocates the separate existing stair module to the opposite end; this is a proposed reuse adjustment, not a new stair asset.

A — REAR-WALL HALL
Centre the riser against the rear long wall, facing the central opening. This is the clearest front-door presentation and gives a balanced facade/interior composition. It has a shallower audience rectangle and concentrates guest arrival opposite the stage, making the front cross-aisle important. Use the same front-left exit, and keep both side aisles clear. Also requires occupied wall/roof fading at every camera orientation. 01-rear-wall-hall-v2.png is the final A image; v1 is retained only as an iteration record.

COMMON APPEARANCE / SHELTER
Retain limestone blocks, olive slate roof, dark timber gables, oak sliding doors and the recognisable long barn mass. Beautification is restrained: clean usable floor and threshold, parked sliding doors around an approximately 8 m central opening, a modest timber BARN STAGE sign, a few warm lights tucked into the interior, and clearly visible exit framing. No truss, new wing, marquee attached to the barn or unrelated venue features. Any sliding-door pockets must clear the new exit and its approach.
All proposed viewing ground and the stage sit inside the roof envelope. The external front apron and central track are NOT sheltered viewing space. Keep the track clear rather than treating it as crowd overflow. Rain protection is a future simulation integration requirement; transparent visuals do not themselves confer weather shelter. No capacity, admission, price, tier or heavy-rain balance outcome is assigned here.

REQUIRED EXTENSIONS TO MARQUEE CONTRACT
1. Include opaque WALLS and gable infill, not just roof/canvas. Suggested separate material group Barn_WallFade and an explicit selector extension; do not pretend the current helper already handles walls or rename all masonry _Roof to conceal the difference.
2. Trigger on ANY relevant person inside the barn's actual interior footprint: audience, performers and staff, including a lone musician before guests arrive. Marquee Sheltering alone is not that count. Keep selection as a second reason to reveal the interior, matching the existing marquee interaction.
3. Split broad wall panels, roof slopes, gable infill and occluding parked door leaves into fadeable meshes/materials. Keep floor, stage, people, instruments, sparse corner/door posts and sign opaque. Avoid leaving heavy opaque rafters across performer heads. Current monolithic shell needs partitioning so floor/frames do not fade with the stone walls. Retain roof shape and full shelter shadow; do not remove roof panels or use an arbitrary camera-only cutaway.
4. Thin visible surfaces and careful partitioning matter: overlapping 30% roof and wall layers combine to roughly 51% tint; duplicate inner/outer masonry and slate layers can be much worse. Native alpha sorting and all four game views must be checked after selection. Keep the same .30 target and timings initially. Any lower-alpha or view-dependent departure would need to be stated explicitly if testing demonstrates it necessary.
5. Replace the current all-building pick box with suitable frame/edge pick targets so the visible people can be selected. Authoritative walk blocking is separate: open only proposed floor, door, stair and access cells while retaining wall boundaries. Fading must not make walls physically passable.

- Preserve outer footprint; shape the visible waterline/bank WITHIN it. Avoid
  adding inaccessible-looking water beyond existing blocked cells.
- Replace uniform orange-brown ring with quiet grey-brown mud, sage grassy
  transition and uneven narrow exposed bank. Nearly flush, not a raised bowl.
- Sparse small rounded faceted stones in groups, not a continuous stone rim.
- A few low reed clusters and sparse lily pads; simplify the current reed fence.
  Concentrate planting at existing planted side. Keep arena-facing lip open.
  Artwork reed height is illustrative: aim low, roughly 0.3–0.5 m for most tufts,
  and check all four game cameras before accepting taller accents. No new trees.
- Shared opaque matte palette for bank/rocks/plants/ducks; separate water
  material as today. Reuse small stone/reed meshes. No microtexture requirement.
- Duck silhouette: rounded faceted body, short neck/head, ochre bill, simple
  tail and eye; green drake head / warm brown alternative, broad colour blocks.
  Keep approximately current half-metre body scale. Closeup is magnified.

INEXPENSIVE RUNTIME MOTION PROPOSAL (NOT IMPLEMENTED)
- Split existing combined Ducks into two independent transform roots, with
  shared base geometry and palette variants. A full skeleton is not needed
  for slow translation/yaw and subtle whole-body bobbing.
- Slow short asymmetric glide segments, eased turns, occasional stillness and
  tiny vertical/pitch bob; different timing/phase for each duck. Avoid circular
  conveyor motion, synchronized pairs or sharp turns. Confine paths to a safe
  inset of existing water polygon and keep away from reeds/pads and each other.
  This is cosmetic path playback, not feeding/needs or simulation AI.
- Small low-opacity trailing wake marks following moving ducks; fade when
  stopped. Optional occasional gentle ripple arc during bob, no splash particles.
- One restrained water material: muted blue-green base, broad shallow colour
  drift/highlight variation. Use cheap local highlight/normal modulation if
  available; no planar reflection camera, SSR dependency, refraction or new
  render pipeline. Concept reflections are stylized colour cues, not a promise
  of mirrored buildings/trees. Avoid constant glitter or high-contrast ripples.
- ALL cosmetic motion uses one explicitly pause-controlled game-time input:
  duck transforms, bob, wakes, fades and shader phase freeze together on pause.
  Do not assume a wall-clock shader time uniform stops with game simulation.
  Resume continuously without elapsed-time jumps. Verify actual pause mechanism.

DAIRY COW — CONCEPT ONLY — 2026-10-05

One shared adult cow design for random arrivals and Council Favours. No event
costs, quantities, odds, effects or mechanics are specified by this artwork.
No Blender mesh, rig, animation, GLB or game implementation has been created.
Coordinator visual review is required before modelling.

Direction: friendly, mildly comic hornless dairy cow; crafted matte low-poly
English farm style. Cream and charcoal broad coat patches, subdued pink muzzle,
dark split hooves. Optional warm-brown recolour should reuse the same mesh.
Humour comes from curious head/ear poses, not oversized eyes or mascot anatomy.

Sheet includes main three-quarter, side/front/rear construction guidance,
adult attendee scale, stand/walk/alert studies, palette and distance-read inset.
AI concept views are guidance, not exact orthographic blueprints; resolve patch
continuity and joint locations in the eventual model. The distance inset is
illustrative and does not constitute native Godot readability verification.

Modelling guidance after approval:
- Keep adult proportions: long barrel torso, defined shoulder/hip transition,
  tapered face and broad muzzle. Withers around adult attendee chest height;
  validate against actual attendee meshes rather than copying image pixels.
- Use economical faceted surfaces and broad patches; no fur/noise texture.
  Proposed opaque palette: cream, charcoal, muted pink and dark hoof, with
  brown replacing charcoal for alternate coat. No exact material count promised.
- Keep torso continuous; provide deformable head/neck and four leg chains with
  correct shoulder/hip, carpal/stifle, hock and hoof articulation. Plan weight
  transitions, not visibly detached rigid toy limbs.
- Ears and a short segmented tail chain need independent motion. Hooves can
  remain rigid weighted ends; muzzle/eyes and restrained udder need no complex
  facial or secondary rig initially. Callouts indicate regions, not bone pivots.
- Standing: four hooves planted. Walking: deliberate weight transfer, no sliding.
  Alert/agitated: raised head, redirected ears, braced stance/tail movement;
  no attacking or rearing behaviour implied. These are pose ideas, not clips.
- Later native checks: attendee scale, ground contact, joint deformation, four
  camera orientations and actual gameplay-distance silhouette/coat readability.

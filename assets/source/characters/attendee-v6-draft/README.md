# Attendee v6 — draft rebuild against the approved concepts

30 September 2026. Exploratory rebuild of both neutral attendees in Blender 4.1.1 from `female-attendee-concept-v1/female-concept-target.png` and `attendee-palette-concept-v1/attendee-colour-concept.png`. **Draft for review only**: no runtime GLB, manifest, pose or game file was changed. Male v5 / female v3 and all pose files remain the approved assets until v6 is reviewed.

## What changed vs v5/v3

- Proportions: head scaled up to roughly 6.5 heads (concept read), shorter neck, wider straight-leg trousers, chunkier rounded shoes, thicker forearms and bigger mitten hands.
- Face: wider planar face, tapered jaw with a sloped chin (no dark underside band), larger, wider-set eyes, wedge nose; subtle mouth on the female only (the colour study male has none). Ears visible on the male.
- Female hair: two continuous thickened shells open at the face — a short outer layer with crown volume ending in irregular turned-out points at jaw level, over a longer under layer ending at the shoulders — plus a broad off-centre swept fringe (up to 150 mm wide, lifted off the forehead for a shadow edge) that crosses above the eyes and merges into the side hair at the temple, and a short parting-side framing lock.
- Male hair: short crop with nape coverage and a faceted swept quiff.
- Sleeves: the upper arm inside each sleeve is tee-coloured (`SleeveLining`), so the armpit gap between sleeve and torso reads as fabric, not skin.
- Tee: fitted crew-neck torso with restrained bust/waist shaping (female) or broader, straighter chest (male); short, slightly flared sleeves. The chest patch is a raycast decal 3 mm off the tee surface — flush, following the cloth, not a raised plate.

## Contract kept

- Female 1.70 m, male 1.78 m; ground-centred origin; faces +Y in Blender (Godot −Z after glTF export).
- One mesh, one material: the existing `DirectionA adult shared recolour palette` (96×8) with `PaletteUV` at slot centres. Skin 0, mouth 1, tee 3, patch 5, trousers 7, shoes/eyes 9, hair 10 — so the 16 approved colourways work unchanged (see `crowd-colourways-*.png`).
- Named vertex groups per part (Trousers, Shoes, Tee, Patch, Arms, Head, Nose, Eyes, Mouth, Ears, Hair).
- Triangles (body only): female 2,592–2,652, male 1,776–1,836 depending on pose.

## Poses (`poses/`)

Same six presentation states per body as attendee-poses-v2, so they can replace the v2 set without runtime logic changes: `relaxed`, `drink_hold` (beer and soft share one grip), `food_hold`, `drinking` beer / soft, `eating` (tray held, other hand pinching at the mouth).

- Generated by the same script (`POSE` argument), not by deforming the relaxed mesh. Arms use two-bone IK with the relaxed upper-arm/forearm lengths, sleeves follow the posed upper arm, and hands are fitted to the measured prop surfaces: a wrapped cup grip with opposing thumb, a palm-up tray support with a thumb steadying the tray's outer edge, and a pinch at the mouth.
- Props are the unchanged runtime files in `assets/runtime/props/` at real size; the body is scaled, props never are. `REVIEW_PROP_*` objects in the `.blend` files are for review only and are not in the GLBs.
- `attendee_poses_v6_manifest.json` uses the v2 manifest schema (`variants.<sex>[]` with `state`, `product`, `file`, `attachments.<product>.position/rotation_degrees/scale` in Godot root-local space, plus hashes and triangle counts). Attachment positions were cross-checked against the `Attachment_Cup`/`Attachment_Food` nodes inside each exported GLB (≤0.005 mm, rotations exact).
- Beer consumption tilts the cup +55° about X (rim at the lips), as in v2. **Change from v2:** soft consumption now tilts the cup +25° about X. Upright, the 142 mm straw forces the cup body into the upper chest; the tilt keeps the straw tip at the lips and the cup clear. `ApplyGuestPropAnchor` already applies manifest rotations generically, so no code change is needed.
- GLBs are named `lwf_attendee_<sex>_<state>[_<product>]_v2.glb`. Switching the game over means copying them into `game/assets/characters/`, pointing `Main.AttendeePoses.cs` at the v6 manifest and changing the `_v1.glb` suffix in `AttendeePose.File`. Not done here.

## Triangle budget

The old 1,800 figure was a planning guess, not a measured limit. `TECHNICAL_SPEC.md` targets 60 FPS at 50 agents and ≥30 FPS at a 1,200-agent benchmark. 1,200 guests × ~2,500 triangles ≈ 3M triangles — well within any current discrete or recent integrated GPU. The costs that will actually bite first are per-guest draw calls (one `MeshInstance3D` per guest, no MultiMesh yet) and simulation CPU, neither of which depends on triangle count. Working guidance: soft cap ~3,000 triangles per body including held props, spent on silhouette (hair, head, hands) rather than hidden interior. Godot 4's import-time automatic mesh LOD keeps distant guests cheap if needed. Re-check with the 1,200-agent benchmark once v6 is integrated.

## Files

- `build_attendee.py` — fully procedural, parameterised build: `blender -b --python build_attendee.py -- female|male OUT.blend <any blend containing the palette material> [POSE] [PROPS_DIR]`. Writes the `.blend`, a body-only `.glb` and a `.json` manifest entry next to `OUT`.
- `render_sheet.py` — three-quarter/front/side/back/face review renders. `crowd.py` — 26-guest colourway crowd at 38.9° elevation, 12 m and 32 m orthographic spans.
- `female-attendee-v6.blend`, `male-attendee-v6.blend`, `review-*.png`, `compare-vs-concept-and-v5.png`.

POSE / ANCHOR INTENT — PROPOSED, NOT VALIDATED RIG TARGETS
Keep both feet planted; tiny chest breathing and <=1cm weight shift, small intermittent head look. Do not root-slide to reach. Suggested turns: head10–20deg, chest5–10deg, no repeated waving.
Pickup: turn/short reach to a nearby inside-counter item; avoid a full rear-shelf reach through torso. Right hand grips lower-mid cup with neutral wrist; cup remains upright. Tray rests on right upturned palm, left hand may steady rim during pickup. Fingers below tray, never in food.
Offer: short controlled outward reach above countertop, keep elbow bent, shoulder down, torso lean modest (~3–6deg maximum concept intent). Never stretch to a distant queue anchor. Bring product to service edge, not all the way to an unchanged customer's mouth.
Bar cup origin y >=1.195 gives 2cm bottom clearance above1.10 for existing -0.075m cup base; suggested working range1.20..1.30. Existing male idle cup origin1.142 / female1.090 would clip this counter if moved over it unchanged, so do not reuse idle_carry without a raised serve target.
Food chip/curry origin y >=1.280 gives 2cm clearance for -0.025m tray underside above1.235; a higher target1.35..1.50 may be more natural on raised support. Pizza lowest mesh y-0.0388 requires origin>=1.2938 for same clearance. Solve actual arm reach before selecting targets; do not copy generated panel hand coordinates.
Starting layout trials (vendor local, not final anchors): bar root z~0.20..0.25, y0; pickup cup z~0.55, offer z~0.90. Food root z~0.85..0.90, y0.47, pickup tray z~1.15, offer z~1.45. Root x and right-hand offset must be solved per body and counter center. Inspect torso/counter depth and knees for collision. If out of reach, shorten gesture to inside service edge rather than add walking/queue movement.

Both sockets are on RightHand, so cup/food grips need distinct hand/forearm orientation. Current 17-bone rig has no finger articulation: broad low-poly grip, no finger animation requirements.

TRANSACTION / LIFECYCLE CONTRACT FOR LATER IMPLEMENTATION
Cosmetic presentation only. Existing simulation retains service, stock, prices, purchase decisions, queues and all ownership. No staff entity, hiring, needs, wages/cost or queue changes.
Current presentation reads vendor.OwnerId / ServiceTicks and immersion Purchases. Later integrator must inspect the authoritative completed-purchase data and stable event identity; no polling array length alone across save/reload.
Use service state only to anticipate turn/pickup; show offer/release at actual successful transaction. Failed/cancelled/out-of-stock service returns to idle without a fake handoff or sold item. Customer's existing held-item state stays authoritative; release/hide server duplicate and show customer's item at one coordinated event.
Do not delay a sale to finish an animation. If simulation runs faster, trim/collapse cosmetic preamble and finish in bounded time. No accumulated backlog of visual transactions; latest/current presentation, bounded events per vendor.
Idle indefinitely between real orders. Suggested artistic cadence: turn0.25–0.4s, pickup0.3–0.45s, offer0.3–0.5s, retract0.3–0.45s; these must fit existing service timing, not alter it. Optional sparse reset0.6–1.0s after some completed orders, interruptible by service.
Freeze animation playback and all timers/blends during pause, terminal/results state and menu. Resume without advancing hidden time or replaying purchases. Destroy/rebuild cosmetic nodes cleanly with vendor lifecycle/save load; use one cached rig and shared clips/materials, no per-frame mesh rebuilds, new materials or unbounded geometry/event accumulation. Do not modify existing staff/band rigs globally.
Later verification after explicit build approval: both bodies, each held product, four gameplay camera rotations at current/default and close zoom, canopy/hatch occlusion, arm/elbow/prop clearance at sampled clip times, feet planted, pause/menu/terminal freeze, fast-time burst and cancelled-sale behavior. No such runtime validation claimed for this concept package.

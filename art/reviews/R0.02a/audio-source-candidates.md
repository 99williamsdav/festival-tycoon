The user approved and downloaded [Small crowd cheering by misjoc](https://freesound.org/people/misjoc/sounds/400590/) on 24 September 2026. Its Freesound page identifies a 5.663-second WAV recorded at a real concert of about 300 people and displays CC0 1.0. The original is `assets/source/audio/crowd/400590__misjoc__small-crowd-cheering.wav`; the runtime copy is `game/assets/audio/set_start_cheer.wav`. It is a provisional set-start cheer, with playback gain scaled by actual watchers and average enthusiasm. Reassess the apparent crowd size during the next listening test.

The user approved two distinct band-entry recordings on 24 September 2026. [Light Applause by ojosdedurazno](https://freesound.org/people/ojosdedurazno/sounds/391326/) is an 8.666-second CC0 stereo WAV of a small crowd applauding after a show; its original is `assets/source/audio/crowd/391326__ojosdedurazno__light-applause.wav` and its runtime copy is `game/assets/audio/band_entry_polite_applause.wav`. [Applause, small audience by nervousneal](https://freesound.org/people/nervousneal/sounds/500977/) is an 11.125-second CC0 stereo WAV of about 50 people applauding in a club; its original is `assets/source/audio/crowd/500977__nervousneal__applause-small-audience.wav` and its runtime copy is `game/assets/audio/band_entry_enthusiastic_applause.wav`. The user judged the latter too excited for polite applause but appropriate for a mid-level act.

At the first performer's on-stage arrival, the game plays one band-entry cue if at least three listeners are physically in place. Twenty or more watchers averaging at least 65 enthusiasm select the more energetic recording; other qualifying audiences select polite applause. The playback level also scales with watcher count, enthusiasm and camera distance. The cue stops when the set starts; the misjoc cheer is reserved for set start. The original downloads retain Freesound IDs and creator names for provenance while runtime copies use cue-based names.

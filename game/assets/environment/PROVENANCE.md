# M0.06 runtime farm assets

These are build-local copies of the approved, project-owned GLBs in
`assets/runtime/environment`. Their editable Blender sources, technical metadata,
review renders, approval history and full notes remain under
`assets/source/environment` and `art/reviews/M0.06`.

Integrated assets: farmhouse v1, small barn v2, large barn v1, trailer stage v2,
generic service point v2, farm gate posts/leaf v1, hedge straight/gate-end v1,
grass tile v1 and vehicle track v1. The food van is intentionally excluded.

All geometry, flat palette textures and materials are original project work,
authored from Blender primitives and custom polygon meshes with AI-assisted
procedural modelling. No downloaded meshes, photographic textures or third-party
art assets are used. Engine lighting, the selection disc and HUD are code-native.

These GLBs contain presentation meshes only. Stable identity, site position,
orientation, permanence and visible state come from
`Festival.Simulation.LowerWitteringFarmScenario`; mesh/node names are never used
for picking or gameplay identity.
